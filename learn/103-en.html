Volume Footprint

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

A footprint opens a candle to show measured or estimated trading at individual price levels. It helps locate busy levels and directional imbalance.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

A normal candle is a building outline; a footprint lists activity on each floor.

Who developed or popularized it?

成交足迹图家族；唯一首创者未核实 / Footprint chart family

Multiple order-flow vendors provide price-level volume charts. Footprint has also been used as a product name; the generic label does not establish a sole inventor.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Switch the chart type to Volume Footprint.
  2. Read what each side displays and how price rows are grouped.
  3. Zoom into a completed candle and compare rows with total volume.
  4. Treat imbalance as unequal activity and check location and subsequent price behavior.

The mistake to avoid

Coverage or classifications can be estimated. A number at one level cannot identify an institution.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Find Volume Footprint in the chart-type menu and check plan and symbol coverage.
  2. Zoom into one completed candle. Read the settings for column definitions, row size and colors before comparing both sides at a price. Compare row totals with candle volume instead of guessing trade direction from color.

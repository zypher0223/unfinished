Hardware Wallets, Multisig & Account Abstraction

My learning & reviewBeginner routeLesson quiz

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

A hardware wallet protects against the key being copied; it cannot judge whether a transaction is safe. If you approve a malicious transaction on the device, the assets still leave, so check the address and contents on the device screen. Write the recovery phrase (the words that restore the whole wallet) on paper and keep it offline, not on a computer or cloud drive. Multisig spreads the risk across keys: 2-of-3 means any 2 of 3 keys can move funds, so losing one is survivable and stealing one is not enough; the costs are more steps, higher fees and trust in the multisig contract itself. ERC-4337 introduces roles such as the UserOperation, bundler, EntryPoint and paymaster; a paymaster can pay fees on the user’s behalf. EIP-7702, activated in Ethereum’s Pectra upgrade, lets an ordinary account delegate its execution logic to a contract; that enables new features, and it also means that being tricked into signing an authorisation pointing to a malicious contract can cost more than a single ordinary approval.

DefinitionAn ordinary wallet account (an externally owned account, EOA) is controlled entirely by one private key; losing or leaking it is hard to undo. There are three more advanced approaches: a hardware wallet keeps the key on an offline device and signs there; a multisig wallet such as Safe needs at least M of N signers to approve a transfer; and account abstraction makes the wallet itself a smart-contract account that can enforce rules such as limits, recovery methods or fees paid by someone else. ERC-4337 (account abstraction) and EIP-7702 (letting an ordinary account delegate to contract code) are both Final in the official Ethereum repository; the former is a formal standard.
First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

A hardware wallet is a key kept inside a safe and only used inside it; multisig is a vault that opens when 2 of 3 keys turn together; account abstraction is a smart lock with rules such as limits, spare keys and guest codes.

A made-up example

Illustration only: a 2-of-3 multisig keeps one key on a phone, one on a hardware wallet and one as a backup held by family. Lose the phone: 2 keys remain and 2 ≥ 2, so transfers still work. Lose two at once: 1 remains and 1 < 2, so the funds are stuck. With 3-of-3, losing one leaves 2 and 2 < 3, also stuck. So leave room for lost keys and store them apart.

Who developed or popularized it?

ERC-4337 was proposed in 2021 and EIP-7702 in 2024; both are Final

ERC-4337 achieves account abstraction through a separate transaction pool without changing Ethereum’s consensus layer; EIP-7702 is a core protocol change letting ordinary accounts set delegated code, which ethereum.org says shipped with the Pectra upgrade. Safe is an open-source smart-account stack with configurable signing thresholds.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Tier by purpose: a hot wallet for small daily amounts, a hardware wallet or multisig for large long-term holdings.
  2. Write the recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline; no photos, no computer or cloud copies, and never tell anyone.
  3. Choose M and N for a multisig with room for lost keys, and spread keys across places and devices.
  4. Check the recipient and contents on the device screen every time; do not sign what you cannot read, including “upgrade your account” authorisations.

The mistake to avoid

Believing a hardware wallet makes you scam-proof. It only stops the key being copied; if you approve a malicious transaction or authorisation on the screen, the assets still go.

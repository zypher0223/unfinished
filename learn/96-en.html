Fibonacci

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

Choose a clear price move and divide its distance into ratios. Retracements measure a return inside that move; extensions project distances beyond it.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

After climbing 100 stairs, walking back 38 is roughly a 38% retracement. The stair does not promise a rescue.

Who developed or popularized it?

Leonardo Fibonacci（数学来源）；交易应用首创者未确认

Leonardo Fibonacci is associated with the mathematical sequence, not the invention of this trading tool. The first trading application has not been verified.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Choose clear start and end points on one timeframe.
  2. Use Fib Retracement and click low then high for an upward example.
  3. Check which end is 0% and which is 100%, then read the ratio prices.
  4. For Trend-Based Fib Extension click the start, end and pullback points.
  5. Observe actual reactions and define when the idea fails.

The mistake to avoid

The common 50% level is not a Fibonacci ratio. Moving endpoints after the fact can fit almost anything.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Expand the Fibonacci drawing group in TradingView’s left toolbar and select Fib Retracement. For an upswing, click its starting low and ending high.
  2. Check the 0% and 100% ends. For a move from 100 to 120, 50% is 110. Select the drawing and inspect its level settings; do not move endpoints merely to fit later prices.

Consensus & Chain Types

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

Without a bank as referee, the core problem is spending the same coin twice (double spending). Consensus uses economic incentives to make honest bookkeeping pay better than cheating: PoW makes cheating cost huge amounts of electricity and hardware, PoS makes cheaters lose their own staked coins.

DefinitionA consensus mechanism is the set of rules plus rewards and penalties by which mutually distrustful nodes decide who writes the next block and what counts as valid. Proof of work (PoW) competes for the right to write by spending computing power, so an attacker needs most of the hash power; proof of stake (PoS) gives block-producing chances to those who lock up tokens, and misbehaviour is punished by slashing the stake. By who may write and who may read, chains are public, consortium or private.
First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

PoW is like a quiz where whoever solves the puzzle first writes the page, and cheating means buying most of the machines; PoS is like a deposit scheme where the bookkeeper posts a bond and forfeits it for bad entries.

A made-up example

Illustration only: on a PoS chain you stake 32 tokens to become a validator and earn rewards for producing blocks on time; if you sign two conflicting blocks, the protocol slashes part of your stake and ejects you from the validator set.

Who developed or popularized it?

Proof of work grew out of 1990s anti-spam research and was first used for a public chain by Bitcoin; proof of stake was first put into practice by Peercoin in 2012

Using computational cost to deter abuse appears in Dwork and Naor (1992) and Adam Back’s Hashcash (1997); Bitcoin (2009) applied it to decide who writes the ledger. Peercoin adopted proof of stake in 2012, and Ethereum moved from PoW to PoS in September 2022. The Byzantine Generals Problem, posed by Lamport and colleagues in 1982, is the classic model of distributed consensus.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Check which consensus the chain uses: PoW, PoS or a variant.
  2. See whether validators or mining pools are concentrated; the larger the top few, the higher the risk of collusion or censorship.
  3. Separate public, consortium and private chains; the latter two need permission and resemble company databases.
  4. Before staking, read the slashing rules and the exit waiting time.

The mistake to avoid

Treating PoS as a savings product. Staking rewards pay for doing validation work and bearing slashing risk, and a price drop during the lock-up still means a loss.

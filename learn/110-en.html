Liquidations

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

An exchange may close or reduce a leveraged position when collateral is insufficient. This records liquidations that happened, not a heatmap predicting future liquidation levels.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

It is a log of past fire alarms, not a prediction of the next fire.

Who developed or popularized it?

交易所风险控制事件数据；单一创始人未确认 / Exchange risk-event data

Liquidations are recorded outcomes of leveraged-contract risk controls, not a formula attributable to a single indicator inventor.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Separate actual liquidation volume from estimated future liquidation maps.
  2. Match exchange, contract and time window.
  3. Understand that closing longs generally involves selling and closing shorts buying back.
  4. Compare price and OI to study forced-position reductions.

The mistake to avoid

Public feeds can be incomplete or sampled. Large liquidations do not ensure a reversal.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Open historical liquidation events, select a fixed window and exchange, and distinguish recorded events from predicted liquidation heatmaps.
  2. Read long and short liquidations separately, checking currency units and coverage. Compare price and OI over the same period and record timing without assuming a reversal.

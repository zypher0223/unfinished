Long/Short Accounts Ratio

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

This compares the number of net-long accounts with net-short accounts in a defined sample. It counts accounts, not their money.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

In a one-person-one-vote poll, a large account and a tiny account may each count once.

A made-up example

Illustration only: 60 net-long accounts divided by 40 net-short accounts gives 1.5. One large short may outweigh many small longs.

Who developed or popularized it?

交易平台统计口径；单一创始人未确认 / Exchange-defined statistic

Long/short account ratios are platform-defined account statistics. No verified universal sole inventor is established.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Read whether the sample is all accounts, top traders or position value.
  2. Match the exchange, time window and sample before comparing.
  3. Interpret a value above 1 only as more net-long accounts in that sample.
  4. Compare with price and OI without treating account counts as capital size.

The mistake to avoid

Account ratio is not position-size ratio, and it does not mean total long contracts exceed total short contracts.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Open the provider’s derivatives statistics and select exchange, pair and account-count ratio. A top-trader position-size ratio is a different statistic.
  2. At one timestamp record long and short account shares, and check whether the display is percentages or their quotient. Compare that observation with price at the same time.

Open Interest

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

Open interest counts contracts that remain open. It is not total daily trading volume and does not mean longs outnumber shorts.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

Volume is today's cinema entries and exits; open interest is the tickets still active now.

Who developed or popularized it?

交易所合约统计；无已核实单一创始人 / Exchange contract statistic

Open interest is a basic derivatives-market statistic, not a proprietary indicator invention. A first user has not been verified.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Choose a derivative contract rather than assuming spot has OI.
  2. Check whether units are contracts, coins or dollar value.
  3. Compare OI with price without interpreting every rise as bullish.
  4. Match exchange and contract; dollar-valued OI also changes with price.

The mistake to avoid

Each contract has a long and a short. Rising OI does not establish the direction of new risk.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Choose a supported futures or perpetual contract, then search Indicators for Open Interest. A spot instrument itself has no derivatives open interest.
  2. Place the cursor on the same bar to read price and OI together. Record whether OI is contracts, coins or notional value before comparing adjacent bars.

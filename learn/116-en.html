Dow Theory

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

Dow Theory separates the main trend from shorter reactions and looks for confirmation across related markets. The classic stock-market framework compares industrial and transport averages.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

A rising tide contains retreating waves; one retreat does not prove the tide has turned.

A made-up example

Illustration only: industrials make a new high while transports do not. That is non-confirmation, not an immediate short order.

Who developed or popularized it?

Charles H. Dow；后由 Nelson、Hamilton、Rhea 等整理

The foundation comes from Dow's newspaper editorials; Nelson, Hamilton, Rhea and others systematized it later. Dow did not publish today's complete formulation himself.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Identify major rising or falling swings on a larger timeframe.
  2. Describe short reactions separately from the primary trend.
  3. For the classic US-stock approach compare key breaks in industrial and transport averages.
  4. Keep the existing trend as a testable hypothesis until clear contrary evidence arrives.

The mistake to avoid

Crypto lacks a natural industrial/transport equivalent. Two arbitrary coins do not recreate classic confirmation.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Find the industrial and transportation indices you intend to study and compare them using matching timeframes.
  2. Mark previous highs/lows and breakout times on both charts before checking confirmation. A crypto-market analogy is an adaptation, not the original definition.

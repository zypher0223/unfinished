TPO/Market Profile

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

TPO splits a session into time blocks and records which price levels each block visits. It primarily measures time opportunities, not traded volume.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

Count how many time slots shoppers visit each floor, rather than how many goods they buy.

Who developed or popularized it?

J. Peter Steidlmayer；Chicago Board of Trade

Wiley credits Steidlmayer with initiating Market Profile and the Liquidity Data Bank at CBOT. Later implementations have multiple contributors.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Choose the Time Price Opportunity chart type and start with daily sessions and 30-minute blocks.
  2. Read a row as the number of time blocks visiting that price.
  3. Find the widest area and the value-area boundaries.
  4. Compare concentrated balanced sessions with stretched directional sessions.

The mistake to avoid

More TPOs do not necessarily mean more volume. Session, time zone and row-size settings matter.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Open the chart-type menu and look for Time Price Opportunity. It is a chart type rather than an ordinary indicator line; Volume Profile is not an equivalent fallback.
  2. Check session, time zone, time block and row size in settings. Count the blocks visiting one price row, then compare two sessions without changing those settings.

Smart Money Concepts (SMC)

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

SMC often combines prior highs/lows, fast price moves and structural changes into a chart-reading process. Smart money is an interpretive label, not direct visibility into institutions' accounts.

First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

Footprints suggest a route but do not prove who walked it.

Who developed or popularized it?

多来源现代标签；与 Michael J. Huddleston 教学有关

SMC is not one standardized theory. Many terms overlap with ICT teaching, but a sole inventor of the whole SMC family is not reliably established.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Write the chosen definitions of swings, BOS and order blocks.
  2. Mark obvious prior highs and lows before seeing the outcome.
  3. After a fast move or failed break wait for your defined structure and retest conditions.
  4. Record qualifying failures in replay as well as successes.

The mistake to avoid

Candles cannot prove that institutions must refill a zone or that resting orders exist there. Complex labels do not establish an edge.

A concrete practice sequence

  1. Mark prior highs/lows with horizontal lines, then draw areas under a named model and annotate the selection rule.
  2. Separate a brief sweep-and-return from a structural change. Record failed areas rather than treating a rectangle as proof of institutional orders.

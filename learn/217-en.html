New Token Standards: ERC-404, 6551, 3525

My learning & reviewBeginner routeLesson quiz

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

On Ethereum, ERC-20 defines divisible fungible tokens while ERC-721 defines unique, indivisible non-fungible tokens (see the "Smart Contracts & Token Approvals" lesson on this site). Three newer standards address their limitations in distinct ways: first, ERC-404 is an experimental, unofficial community standard that has NOT passed the formal Ethereum EIP process, linking whole fungible token balances to automatic NFT minting and burning to provide instant AMM liquidity; second, EIP-6551 (Token Bound Accounts), which is in the EIP process but still at Review status, giving every ERC-721 NFT its own smart contract wallet so it can hold assets and interact with dApps; third, EIP-3525 (Semi-Fungible Tokens), a Final standard, using an "ID + Slot + Value" structure to represent financial instruments like bonds or vouchers that share terms (Slot) while having splittable balances (Value). Currently, their bottlenecks include high gas overhead and unaudited custom implementations in ERC-404, front-running fraud when trading nested assets inside ERC-6551 accounts, and limited native UI support across wallets and DEXs (see the "DEXs & AMMs" lesson on this site) for ERC-3525.

DefinitionEmerging token architectures building upon ERC-20 and ERC-721 to explore hybrid fungible/non-fungible liquidity (ERC-404), Token Bound Accounts (ERC-6551), and semi-fungible financial instruments (ERC-3525).
First chart ever? Read these four ideas first
Price chart and candle
A candle summarizes one chosen time interval. On a daily chart, each candle is one day; it shows the opening, highest, lowest and closing price for that day.
Length or period
A length of 14 usually means 14 candles, not automatically 14 days. On a one-hour chart that means 14 hours of bars.
Main chart and lower pane
The main chart shows price. A lower pane is a separate little chart below it. Its numbers may use completely different units.
Signal
A signal is a condition worth checking, such as one line crossing another. It is an observation, not a guarantee or an automatic order.

Think of it this way

If ERC-20 is divisible cash and ERC-721 is an indivisible collector's stamp, ERC-404 is like a voucher that automatically issues a stamp whenever you hold a whole unit and shreds the stamp if you spend a fraction; ERC-6551 gives each stamp its own built-in lockbox; and ERC-3525 acts like reloadable gift cards from the same batch that can be split or merged by value.

A made-up example

Suppose a user holds 1.5 ERC-404 tokens, automatically receiving 1 linked NFT. Selling 0.6 tokens on a DEX drops the balance to 0.9 (below 1 whole unit), causing the contract to burn the linked NFT. Conversely, if a user deposits 50 USDC into a game character NFT's ERC-6551 Token Bound Account and transfers the character NFT to a buyer, control of the 50 USDC moves automatically with the NFT (for illustrative purposes only).

Who developed or popularized it?

From EIP-3525 (2020) and EIP-6551 (2023) to the ERC-404 (2024) Experiment

EIP-3525 was proposed in December 2020 by the Solv Protocol team and later reached Final status to standardize semi-fungible financial tokens. EIP-6551 was authored in February 2023 by teams including Future Primitive, using a singleton registry to assign deterministic smart contract accounts to existing ERC-721 NFTs without modifying their contracts. ERC-404 was launched in February 2024 by the Pandora team as an unofficial experimental hybrid contract.

An attributed developer, popularizer and first user are different claims. A unique first user is stated only when reliable documentation supports it.

Read it one step at a time

  1. Identify the standard status: check on a block explorer whether the contract implements a Final EIP (ERC-3525), an EIP still under review (ERC-6551), or an experimental design outside the EIP process (ERC-404).
  2. Check ERC-404 burn priority: before transferring fractional ERC-404 balances, verify which linked NFT ID will be burned so rare traits are not accidentally destroyed.
  3. Verify ERC-6551 nested balances: when buying an NFT with a Token Bound Account, ensure the marketplace verifies state roots so the seller cannot front-run and drain its contents.
  4. Inspect ERC-3525 Slot and Value fields: confirm both the Slot (maturity, yield tier, or category) and Value (face amount) of semi-fungible tokens.
  5. Assess gas costs and security audits: complex state updates consume higher gas and require thorough independent contract audits.

The mistake to avoid

Misconception 1: Assuming every "ERC" label implies formal Ethereum approval—ERC-404 has not passed the official EIP review process. Misconception 2: Accidentally burning a rare NFT by letting an ERC-404 token balance dip below a whole integer during a transfer. Misconception 3: Buying an ERC-6551 NFT without protection against sellers front-running the sale to withdraw nested tokens.

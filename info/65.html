稳定币定期储备与鉴证报告（Stablecoin Reserve and Attestation Reports）是由锚定法币的稳定币发行商定期披露的储备资产明细说明，以及由独立第三方会计师事务所出具的鉴证报告（Attestation Report）。它用于向市场展示：在特定截止时点，发行商账上究竟持有哪些具体的美元资产来支撑流通中的稳定币负债，并记录独立执业会计师对管理层声明所做的核验程序。

English introduction

Stablecoin Reserve and Attestation Reports are periodic disclosures published by fiat-backed stablecoin issuers (such as the issuers of USDC or USDT) accompanied by independent third-party accountant attestation reports. They show market participants what specific dollar-denominated assets back the circulating stablecoin liabilities as of a stated cutoff timestamp, and document the verification procedures performed by independent certified public accountants on management's assertions.

发布或统计机构
稳定币发行商与独立鉴证会计师；以Circle USDC为例
频率与时间
按发行商月度或季度 / Issuer-specific monthly or quarterly · 报告覆盖日与正式出具日不同；Circle有周度披露及月度鉴证。

这项数据从哪里来？

USDC于2018年推出，Circle自推出后提供月度储备鉴证；2022年进一步披露储备细节。不同稳定币报告制度不同，不存在统一的全球首发日。

USDC launched in 2018 with monthly reserve attestations; disclosures expanded in 2022. Other stablecoins follow different reporting histories.

English written lesson

Think of a stablecoin attestation report like a prepaid voucher company hiring an independent accountant to inspect its vaults on a specific date: the accountant checks both how many $1 vouchers are circulating outside (liabilities) and whether the vault holds at least as much cash and immediately cashable short-term Treasury bills (reserves), rather than illiquid long-term IOUs.

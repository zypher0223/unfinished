欧洲央行利率决议（ECB Monetary Policy Decision）由欧洲中央银行管理委员会（Governing Council）按日程于法兰克福时间周四下午14:15发布政策声明，14:45由欧洲央行行长举行新闻发布会，并在3月、6月、9月、12月季度会议同步公布欧元区宏观经济预测（Staff Projections）。它决定整个欧元区20个成员国商业银行与央行之间的三大官方政策利率及资产负债表安排。新手最常犯的错误是分不清欧洲央行的“三大政策利率”里究竟哪一个是市场核心锚定基准，。

English introduction

The ECB Monetary Policy Decision, announced eight times a year by the European Central Bank Governing Council on scheduled Thursdays at 14:15 Frankfurt time followed by the President's press conference at 14:45 (with Macroeconomic Staff Projections released in March, June, September, and December), sets official policy interest rates and balance-sheet parameters for the 20-nation euro area. It governs funding costs for the world's second-largest reserve currency. Beginners often confuse the ECB's three different policy rates or assume an ECB rate cut automatically boosts USD-denominated U.S. stocks and Bitcoin.

这项数据从哪里来？

ECB于1998年成立，欧元区统一货币政策于1999年开始；这是欧元体系政策起点，不是欧洲首次出现利率。

The ECB was established in 1998 and single monetary policy began in 1999, not the beginning of European interest rates.

English written lesson

Whereas the Federal Reserve sets the price of U.S. dollar funding, the ECB sets the price of euro funding; if the ECB cuts euro rates faster than the Fed cuts dollar rates, euro borrowing gets cheaper, but global capital may rotate out of lower-yielding euros into higher-yielding dollars, pushing the U.S. Dollar Index (DXY) up.

English introduction

Discount Window Borrowing (focused primarily on Primary Credit in the Federal Reserve's H.4.1 release) measures short-term collateralized loans extended directly by the Federal Reserve to eligible depository institutions against pledged loans or securities. Serving as the central bank's standing liquidity safety valve (lender-of-last-resort backstop) for commercial banks, it is an institutional funding facility rather than a retail lending counter for households or non-bank corporations.

Discount-window lending dates to the Fed’s early operations in 1914; the modern primary-credit program began in 2003.

English written lesson

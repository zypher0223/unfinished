Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey PMMS

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

PMMS reports average rates for a defined mortgage sample, commonly the 30-year fixed mortgage, not the central bank policy rate.

Start with an everyday comparison

The same home costs more each month when the installment financing rate rises.

What does this number measure?

Rates are annual percentages; 6% to 6.5% is 50 basis points. Borrower credit, down payment and fees change individual quotes.

Why can markets react?

Mortgage costs affect housing and spending and reflect Treasury yields plus mortgage spreads; distinguish disinflation from recession-driven declines.

Read the release step by step

  1. Check the official reference period, publication date, unit and seasonal adjustment.
  2. Read the named headline and its components; distinguish levels, changes and revised observations.
  3. Compare actual, independently sourced consensus and revised prior values; do not manufacture a consensus if none is available.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

An orderly decline with healthy jobs may improve affordability and risk valuations.

When a fall is plausible

Persistent increases hurt demand, while a crisis-driven collapse can coincide with falling stocks and crypto.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

A small move already priced in bonds may have little additional impact.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical $300k, 30-year mortgage: principal and interest are about $1,799 at 6% versus $1,996 at 7%, excluding taxes and insurance. That extra cost can squeeze other spending.

History and origin

PMMS began in 1971. In 2022 it moved to application-based methodology; back-calculated data should not be confused with the original survey method.

Where and how to find the data

Freddie Mac · Weekly · Usually Thursday at noon U.S. Eastern; a Thursday holiday may move it earlier.

Freddie Mac official data

  1. Open the latest PMMS weekly release.
  2. Choose the 30-Year Fixed Rate and its historical chart.
  3. Open Methodology to check the observation week and borrower criteria; this is not an individualized quote.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. A policy cut does not guarantee an equal mortgage-rate drop; long yields and spreads matter.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

Household Loan Delinquency Rates

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

A delinquency rate measures overdue loan balances in a defined portfolio, not the percentage of all households going bankrupt.

Start with an everyday comparison

If $3 of a $100 loan portfolio is overdue, the balance-based rate is 3%; that need not mean three borrowers out of 100.

What does this number measure?

Board delinquency includes loans 30 days past due and still accruing plus nonaccrual loans. Charge-offs and New York Fed 90-day transition rates are different measures.

Why can markets react?

Stress can reduce spending and bank lending, lowering earnings and risk appetite. Broad crypto effects are indirect and quarterly figures lag events.

Read the release step by step

  1. Select a loan type.
  2. Check overdue-day threshold, denominator and stock versus transition definition.
  3. Compare several quarters with employment and lending standards.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

Falling delinquency with stable jobs may reduce credit fears.

When a fall is plausible

Broad deterioration with job losses can damage consumption, banks and risk assets.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

An anticipated small move or a technical reporting change may have little effect.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: $30bn overdue out of $1tn equals 3%; $33bn out of $1.1tn is also 3%. Higher overdue dollars alone do not show a worse rate.

History and origin

These are supervisory credit-risk statistics rather than a trader invention. The Board series extends into the 1980s; the New York Fed household panel is a separate dataset.

Where and how to find the data

Federal Reserve Board; New York Fed as a separate supplementary source · Quarterly · Quarterly; the Board and New York Fed reports have separate schedules and definitions.

Federal Reserve Board; New York Fed as a separate supplementary source official data

  1. Under Delinquency rates select All → SA.
  2. Locate Credit cards, Other consumer loans or Residential real estate; keep one category for comparisons.
  3. Read About to confirm the overdue threshold; do not substitute charge-off rates or New York Fed transition rates.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. Charge-offs and denominator changes can improve the ratio without improving borrowers.
  2. Do not mix different samples or delinquency thresholds.

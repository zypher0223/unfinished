Federal Reserve FOMC Rate Decision

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

The FOMC sets a target range for overnight federal funds and communicates its outlook, rather than setting equity returns or a universal mortgage rate.

Start with an everyday comparison

It adjusts a foundation of dollar funding costs that transmits through banks and bonds.

What does this number measure?

25bp equals 0.25 percentage point. The target range, effective rate and SOFR differ; unchanged rates can still surprise through guidance.

Why can markets react?

Rates affect debt costs, valuation discounting, dollar demand and crypto funding. Disinflationary cuts differ from emergency recession cuts.

Read the release step by step

  1. Check the official reference period, publication date, unit and seasonal adjustment.
  2. Read the named headline and its components; distinguish levels, changes and revised observations.
  3. Compare actual, independently sourced consensus and revised prior values; do not manufacture a consensus if none is available.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

A more accommodative path than expected with resilient growth can support risk assets.

When a fall is plausible

A hawkish surprise or an emergency cut revealing deep weakness can hurt markets.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

An expected cut paired with fewer future cuts can cause whipsaws.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: a fully expected 25bp cut comes with guidance for one future cut rather than three priced in. The surprise can still be hawkish.

History and origin

The FOMC framework developed through the 1933 and 1935 banking legislation; immediate policy announcements began in 1994, not policy decisions themselves.

Where and how to find the data

Federal Reserve Board · Usually eight scheduled meetings yearly · Usually statement at 2:00 p.m. and press conference at 2:30 p.m. U.S. Eastern; unscheduled meetings differ.

Federal Reserve Board official data

  1. Choose the latest meeting in the calendar.
  2. Open Statement for the federal funds target range, then Implementation Note.
  3. If available, read Projection Materials, the dot plot and the complete Press Conference questions and answers.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. The dot plot is a conditional projection, not a promise; read the complete communication.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

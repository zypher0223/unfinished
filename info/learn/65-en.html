What does this number measure?

Read dollar reserves, liabilities and coverage; point-in-time attestation is not a full financial-statement audit or a guarantee of future sufficiency.

Why can markets react?

Backing and redemption confidence affect crypto settlement and collateral; gaps or illiquidity can spread through leverage. Routine reports rarely drive broad equities directly.

Read the release step by step

  1. Check the official reference period, publication date, unit and seasonal adjustment.
  2. Read the named headline and its components; distinguish levels, changes and revised observations.
  3. Compare actual, independently sourced consensus and revised prior values; do not manufacture a consensus if none is available.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

Evidence resolving prior doubts and restoring normal redemptions may improve crypto confidence.

When a fall is plausible

Shortfalls, qualified conclusions, illiquid assets or unexplained delays may trigger depegging and deleveraging.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

A routine timely report with no surprises may simply preserve confidence.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: $10bn liabilities and $10.1bn reserves imply 101% coverage, but $3bn temporarily inaccessible cash can still create redemption strain.

History and origin

USDC launched in 2018 with monthly reserve attestations; disclosures expanded in 2022. Other stablecoins follow different reporting histories.

Where and how to find the data

Stablecoin issuers and independent accountants; Circle USDC example · Issuer-specific monthly or quarterly · Observation and report-signing dates differ; Circle has weekly disclosures and monthly reserve examinations.

Stablecoin issuers and independent accountants; Circle USDC example official data

  1. Under Monthly assurance and transparency select the report month.
  2. Download the signed independent accountant report rather than relying only on the summary page.
  3. Check observation dates, circulating liabilities, reserve value, composition, examination scope and the conclusion.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. Attestation is not an audit; corporate audit and reserve examinations answer different questions.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

MBA Weekly Mortgage Applications

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

It tracks applications for purchase mortgages and refinancing, not approvals or closed loans.

Start with an everyday comparison

More exam applications are not more passes; refinancing is not a new homebuyer.

What does this number measure?

Read index levels and weekly percentage changes. Refinancing is highly rate-sensitive and can dominate the composite.

Why can markets react?

Purchase demand and refinancing affect housing and household cash flow; recession-driven rate falls can offset those benefits for broad risk assets.

Read the release step by step

  1. Check the official reference period, publication date, unit and seasonal adjustment.
  2. Read the named headline and its components; distinguish levels, changes and revised observations.
  3. Compare actual, independently sourced consensus and revised prior values; do not manufacture a consensus if none is available.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

A sustained purchase recovery with stable jobs can suggest easing financial pressure.

When a fall is plausible

Persistent purchase weakness with tighter credit can undermine growth expectations.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

A refinancing-only jump or holiday distortion may produce little broad response.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: composite applications rise 10%, purchases fall 2%, refinancing rises 25%. The headline is not a 10% rise in homebuyers.

History and origin

MBA launched the weekly survey in 1990; unadjusted indexes use the week of March 16, 1990 as 100.

Where and how to find the data

Mortgage Bankers Association MBA · Weekly · Usually Wednesday at 7:00 a.m. U.S. Eastern; holidays may change timing.

Mortgage Bankers Association MBA official data

  1. Open the latest Weekly Applications Survey release or MBA Newslink.
  2. Find Market Composite, Purchase and Refinance indexes.
  3. Compare adjusted weekly changes and a multiweek trend; a refinancing jump is not a purchase jump.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. Applications are not originations; holiday adjustments and the refinancing mix matter.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

FHFA House Price Index

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

The common purchase-only series uses purchase mortgages acquired or guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac; coverage differs from Case-Shiller.

Start with an everyday comparison

It is a large defined mortgage sample, not a census of every cash purchase.

What does this number measure?

Separate index levels, adjusted monthly changes and annual growth; monthly purchase-only and quarterly expanded series are not interchangeable.

Why can markets react?

Prices influence collateral, credit and household wealth; crypto receives mostly indirect rate and risk effects.

Read the release step by step

  1. Check the official reference period, publication date, unit and seasonal adjustment.
  2. Read the named headline and its components; distinguish levels, changes and revised observations.
  3. Compare actual, independently sourced consensus and revised prior values; do not manufacture a consensus if none is available.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

Stable collateral with improving affordability can support a soft landing.

When a fall is plausible

Broad declines with loan stress can damage confidence; overheating may raise yields.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

Divergence from Case-Shiller may reflect coverage and timing, leaving investors waiting.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: monthly growth is 0.3% against 0.2% expected, but the prior month is revised from 0.4% to 0.1%. The net news is mixed.

History and origin

OFHEO published official HPI reports in 1996 with estimates back to 1975; publication history and back-series dates differ.

Where and how to find the data

Federal Housing Finance Agency FHFA · Monthly and quarterly · Follow the FHFA calendar, usually 9:00 a.m. U.S. Eastern.

Federal Housing Finance Agency FHFA official data

  1. Open the latest HPI Report.
  2. Select monthly purchase-only or quarterly expanded-data; do not join them without checking definitions.
  3. Read adjusted monthly and annual changes, geographic detail and mortgage coverage.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. Coverage is not all homes; distinguish all-transactions, purchase-only and expanded-data series.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

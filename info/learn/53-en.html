Overnight Reverse Repurchase Facility ON RRP

My learning & reviewBeginner route

Records are saved only in this browser. Reading is not the same as mastery.

Eligible institutions place cash overnight with the Fed through reverse repos; the balance is usage of the facility, not new asset purchases.

Start with an everyday comparison

It is an overnight parking place for institutional cash; fewer parked cars do not identify their next destination.

What does this number measure?

Read accepted dollar amounts and the administered facility rate, distinct from SOFR.

Why can markets react?

Cash can move to bills or bank deposits. It may cushion reserve effects of runoff, but the destination is not automatically bitcoin.

Read the release step by step

  1. Identify domestic ON RRP accepted amount, rate, counterparties and date.
  2. Compare the previous business day and month/quarter ends and relative bill yields.
  3. There is no official consensus field; compare reserves and TGA before inferring the destination of cash.

Three possible market reactions

These are conditional explanations, not fixed trading rules. The same release can be interpreted differently when inflation, recession or funding stress dominates.

When a rise is plausible

Outflows with stable reserves and funding spreads may support smoother conditions.

When a fall is plausible

Flight to safe parking or reserve stress after buffers decline may hurt risk assets.

When a mixed or sideways response is plausible

Relative bill yields and quarter-end adjustments can change usage without moving broad markets.

Work through a made-up example

Hypothetical: RRP falls $50bn while TGA rises $50bn and reserves are unchanged. Bill purchases may explain it; equity inflows are not established.

History and origin

Fixed-rate overnight reverse-repo testing began September 23, 2013; repo transactions themselves are much older.

Where and how to find the data

Federal Reserve Bank of New York · Every U.S. business day · Operation usually 12:45–1:15 p.m. U.S. Eastern, with results afterward; verify current parameters.

Federal Reserve Bank of New York official data

  1. Open Reverse Repo Operations and select results.
  2. Choose Overnight Reverse Repo, not the foreign-official reverse-repo pool.
  3. Record accepted amount, counterparty count, rate and date; compare bill yields and reserve conditions.

Check the current official release calendar. Time zones, daylight saving, holidays and revisions can change what you see; never assume a displayed local time is New York time.

Common mistakes

  1. A fall is not mechanically bullish and a rise is not mechanically bearish.
  2. Markets react to new information relative to expectations. Check yields, the dollar and price confirmation; the release cannot guarantee a trade.

英国央行利率决议（Bank of England MPC Rate Decision）由英格兰银行货币政策委员会（Monetary Policy Committee，简称MPC）按日程于伦敦时间周四中午12:00发布，同步公布《货币政策摘要与会议纪要（Minutes）》，并在2月、5月、8月、11月季度例会一并发布包含通胀与GDP预测的《货币政策报告（MPR）》及行长记者会。它决定英国商业银行存放在英国央行的官方基准利率（Bank Rate）。新手最常忽略的是英国央行9名委员经常出现“三派甚至多派分裂投票”的独特机制，。

English introduction

The Bank of England MPC Rate Decision, announced eight times a year by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) on scheduled Thursdays at 12:00 noon London time alongside the Monetary Policy Summary and Minutes (plus the quarterly Monetary Policy Report and press conference in February, May, August, and November), sets the official Bank Rate for the United Kingdom. It governs sterling funding costs and UK gilt yields. Beginners often look only at whether Bank Rate changed while ignoring the 9-member committee's three-way vote split, which frequently telegraphs the next policy turn.

English written lesson

Unlike central banks that usually vote near-unanimously, the Bank of England's 9-member MPC acts like an open jury roll call: even if the committee votes 5-to-4 to hold rates steady today, a shift from 8-to-1 last month to 5-to-4 today reveals that three members just switched to the rate-cut camp—leaving the next meeting just one vote away from a cut.

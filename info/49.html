房地美初级抵押贷款市场调查利率（Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey，简称PMMS）由美国政府支持企业房地美（Freddie Mac）于每周四中午发布，用来公布美国最主流的“30年期固定利率按揭贷款（30-Year Fixed-Rate Mortgage）”及15年期固定按揭的全国平均利率水平。它是衡量美国家庭买房借钱成本最核心的公开参考标尺，反映的是商业按揭市场的周度平均融资利率，而不是美联储直接设定的联邦基金政策利率。新手最常误以为美联储宣布降息25个基点的第二天，30年期按揭利率就会自动下降25个基点。

English introduction

The Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS), published every Thursday at noon Eastern by Freddie Mac, reports the weekly U.S. national average interest rate on the benchmark 30-Year Fixed-Rate Mortgage (and 15-Year Fixed-Rate Mortgage). It serves as the primary public barometer of U.S. household home-borrowing costs, measuring long-term retail mortgage financing rates rather than the Federal Reserve's overnight policy rate. Beginners often assume that if the Fed cuts its overnight rate by 25 basis points, the 30-year mortgage rate automatically drops by 25 basis points the next day.

这项数据从哪里来？

Freddie Mac自1971年进行PMMS；2022年改为利用贷款申请资料的新方法，回算资料不能与1971年的原调查方法混同。

PMMS began in 1971. In 2022 it moved to application-based methodology; back-calculated data should not be confused with the original survey method.

中文文字教学

如果美联储的联邦基金利率是央行给商业银行的“隔夜批发进货价”，那么30年期按揭利率就是房贷机构卖给普通买房家庭的“30年超长分期零售价”；零售价其实主要跟着10年期美国国债收益率再加上一层风险与提前还款利差走，而不是照搬美联储的隔夜利率。

English written lesson

If the Federal Reserve's federal funds rate is the overnight wholesale rate between banks, the 30-year mortgage rate is the 30-year retail installment rate charged to homebuyers; retail 30-year mortgage rates track the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield plus a mortgage risk/prepayment spread rather than copying the overnight rate.

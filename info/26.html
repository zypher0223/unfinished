English introduction

The S&P US services headline is a business activity index, not a simple average of every service-sector subindex.

频率与时间
每月，初值与终值 · 初值通常月内下旬，终值次月月初，通常东部时间9:45；不是与制造业终值同一天的保证。

这项数据从哪里来？

美国标普服务业PMI历史数据自2009年可得，后来整合到S&P Global体系。本课没有把可回溯的第一期数据当成首次公开发布日，也没有给它虚构个人创始人。

S&P US services PMI history is available from 2009 and later became part of S&P Global’s suite. The first observation is not necessarily the public launch date, and no individual founder is asserted here.

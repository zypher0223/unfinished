English introduction

A household survey of attitudes toward jobs, income and business conditions. It is not a receipt-based spending count.

发布或统计机构
The Conference Board
频率与时间
每月 / Monthly · 通常月末周二10:00美国东部时间；以官方发布日历为准。

这项数据从哪里来？

该机构的消费者调查始于1967年，现行指数以1985年为100；这是调查体系的历史，并不是股市交易规则的发明年份。

The survey began in 1967; the index uses 1985 as its reference value of 100. This is survey history, not the invention of a trading rule.

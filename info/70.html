EIA每周原油库存（EIA Weekly Crude Oil Inventories）由美国能源信息署（U.S. Energy Information Administration）在每周三美东时间10:30发布的《每周石油状况报告（Weekly Petroleum Status Report）》中公布，用来统计截至上周五的美国商业原油库存总量及周度增减桶数。它是全球原油市场交易最活跃、最权威的官方周度供需平衡表数据。新手最常犯的误解，是看到“原油去库存”就无脑当成“全社会汽油消费需求极度火爆”，或者把周二下午的私人API行业估算和周三的官方EIA政府报告混为一谈。

English introduction

EIA Weekly Crude Oil Inventories, published every Wednesday at 10:30 a.m. Eastern by the U.S. Energy Information Administration within the Weekly Petroleum Status Report, track the total stock and weekly net change of U.S. commercial crude oil held in storage (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, SPR) as of the prior Friday. It is the most authoritative high-frequency supply-and-demand balance sheet for the global oil market. Beginners often assume any crude inventory draw means booming consumer gasoline demand, or confuse Tuesday afternoon's private API industry estimate with Wednesday's official EIA government report.

发布或统计机构
U.S. Energy Information Administration EIA
频率与时间
每周 / Weekly · 通常周三10:30美国东部时间，假日可能延后；查Weekly Petroleum Status Report日历。

这项数据从哪里来？

EIA于1977年成立，石油周报体系在能源统计发展中形成；现行库存序列有更早历史，不能把机构成立年当每个分项首发日。

EIA was established in 1977; the weekly reporting system and historical series evolved separately, so agency formation is not every component’s first release.

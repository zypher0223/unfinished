美联储资产负债表由美联储委员会于每周四下午发布，完整列出美联储截至本周三的“资产端”与“负债端”。它是观察美元基础货币源头与央行扩表（QE/救助）、缩表（QT）进度最权威的每周账本。新手最常犯的误解，是以为美联储资产增加100亿美元，就等于印了100亿美元直接流进股市或比特币。

English introduction

The Federal Reserve Balance Sheet (officially the H.4.1 statistical release, 'Factors Affecting Reserve Balances'), published every Thursday at 4:30 p.m. Eastern by the Federal Reserve Board, details the central bank's assets (U.S. Treasury securities, agency MBS, and lending facilities) and liabilities (currency in circulation, bank reserve balances, the Treasury General Account TGA, and the Overnight Reverse Repo facility ON RRP) as of Wednesday. It is the authoritative weekly ledger for tracking Quantitative Easing (QE), Quantitative Tightening (QT), and emergency liquidity operations. Beginners often assume a $10 billion rise in Fed assets means $10 billion was printed and injected straight into stocks or Bitcoin.

这项数据从哪里来？

美联储自成立早期即公布银行状况，H.4.1是长期央行会计统计。2008年量化宽松后总资产特别受关注；2008年不是资产负债表首次出现。

The Fed has published condition statements since its early years; the balance sheet became prominent after 2008 QE, not first invented then.

中文文字教学

它像整个美元金融体系的总中央账房账本：账本左边（资产）记着央行买进了什么债券或借给哪家困难银行多少钱，账本右边（负债）记着央行付出去的钱最终停在了现钞、银行准备金、财政部账户还是逆回购池子里；左边和右边永远严格相等，但钱停在右边哪个抽屉里，决定了它对市场流动性的实际影响。

English written lesson

Think of it as the master ledger of the U.S. dollar system: the left side (Assets) records what bonds the Fed bought or what emergency loans it made to banks, while the right side (Liabilities) shows which drawer that money currently sits in—paper cash, bank reserves, the government's checking account (TGA), or the reverse-repo parking lot; the two sides always balance, but shifting money between drawers changes market liquidity.

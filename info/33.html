这项数据从哪里来？

BEA官方历史资料记载，1938年2月首次发布月度“收入支付（income payments）”估计，是今日月度个人收入统计的前身。消费、税后收入与储蓄随后在国民账户中共同发展；本课未核实每个今日月度或实际子系列的精确首发日，不能把历史覆盖起点当作首发。

BEA history records that its first monthly estimates of income payments to individuals were released in February 1938, the predecessor of today's monthly personal income series. Consumption, disposable income and saving evolved within the accounts; the exact first release of each modern monthly or real subseries has not been verified here.

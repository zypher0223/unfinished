FHFA 房价指数

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

FHFA房价指数（FHFA House Price Index，简称FHFA HPI）由美国联邦住房金融局（Federal Housing Finance Agency）按月及按季度发布，同样采用“重复销售法”追踪同一套独栋住宅的价格变化，但它的基础数据来源于房利美（Fannie Mae）和房地美（Freddie Mac）两家政府支持企业收购或担保的符合标准（Conforming）按揭贷款记录。它衡量的是“使用两房标准按揭贷款购买的独栋住宅”的同质价格变化，覆盖面与Case-Shiller指数明显不同。新手最常犯的错误是不知道它剔除了全现金买房和超额豪宅贷款（Jumbo Loans），或把月度纯购房系列与季度扩展系列混在一起比。

English introduction

The FHFA House Price Index (FHFA HPI), published monthly and quarterly by the Federal Housing Finance Agency, uses a repeat-sales methodology to track single-family home price changes across mortgages acquired or assured by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. It measures constant-quality price changes specifically for homes financed with conforming enterprise mortgages, giving it a very different sample coverage from Case-Shiller (Lesson 46). Beginners often overlook that it excludes all-cash purchases and jumbo mortgages above conforming loan limits, or mix the monthly purchase-only series with quarterly expanded series.

这项数据从哪里来？

前身OFHEO在1996年已发布官方HPI报告，历史估计回溯到1975年；1996年的发布与1975年的样本历史是两回事。

OFHEO published official HPI reports in 1996 with estimates back to 1975; publication history and back-series dates differ.

English written lesson

Whereas Case-Shiller pulls county deed records that include billionaires buying mansions with cash, the FHFA index pulls repeat mortgage files from the desks of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac; thus it acts as a thermometer specifically for middle-class, mortgage-financed single-family homes across all 50 states.

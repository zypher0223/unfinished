ISM 服务业 PMI

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

调查服务业采购管理人员，覆盖商业活动、新订单、就业、交付等。服务业占美国经济很大部分，因此与总体需求及服务通胀有关。

English introduction

Surveys service-sector purchasing managers on activity, orders, employment and deliveries, informing broad demand and service-price pressure.

发布或统计机构
美国供应管理协会 ISM
频率与时间
每月 · 通常每月第三个工作日东部时间10:00；以ISM日历为准。

这项数据从哪里来？

ISM非制造业调查数据自1997年可得，后来演进为今天的服务业报告。1997年是系列覆盖历史，不应不加说明就当作今日复合指数公式首次发布年；本课未核实精确首发日。

ISM non-manufacturing data are available from 1997, later evolving into today’s services report. That historical coverage is not automatically the first release of the current composite headline formula.

English written lesson

Think of the economy as factories plus stores. Orders are the to-do list, production is completed output, surveys are managers’ reports and GDP is the total account. Timing and coverage differ, so these measures are not interchangeable.

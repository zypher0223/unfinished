高级贷款专员意见调查（SLOOS，Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices）是美联储通常每季度开展一次的定性问卷调查，直接向美国本土大型与中小型商业银行及外资银行驻美分支机构的信贷主管提问：过去三个月贵行放贷审批门槛是收紧了还是放宽了？上门申请贷款的客户需求是变强了还是变弱了？需把银行端的“信贷供给标准（Standards）”与客户端的“借贷需求（Demand）”严格分开。

English introduction

The Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices (SLOOS) is a quarterly qualitative survey conducted by the Federal Reserve that asks senior loan officers at domestic and U.S. branches of foreign commercial banks two distinct sets of questions over the past three months: did your bank tighten or ease credit approval standards, and did borrower demand for loans strengthen or weaken? Readers must strictly separate bank-side credit supply standards from customer-side borrowing demand.

这项数据从哪里来？

美联储1964年发起银行贷款行为调查，初期120家银行、季度调查；此后样本和问题变化，1992年以来通常一年四次。

The survey began in 1964 with 120 banks and quarterly reporting; panels and questions evolved, with four surveys a year typical since 1992.

English written lesson

Think of SLOOS as a quarterly check-in with shopkeepers across the country that asks two separate questions: first, whether the shop raised the bar and collateral rules for extending store credit (supply standards), and second, whether more or fewer customers walked in asking to borrow on credit (borrower demand).

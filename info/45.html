成屋签约销售指数（Pending Home Sales Index，简称PHS）由全美房地产经纪人协会（NAR）按月发布，用来追踪美国二手住宅市场里买卖双方“已经签署购房合同、但尚未走完过户交割流程（Pending）”的交易活动变化。由于在美国买二手房从签合同到最终办完按揭过户通常需要1到2个月（4至8周），它是第43课“成屋销售”的直接先行指标。新手最常犯的误解，。

English introduction

The Pending Home Sales Index (PHS), published monthly by the National Association of Realtors (NAR), tracks existing-home transactions where a purchase contract has been signed during the reference month but the deal has not yet closed. Because closing a U.S. existing-home purchase typically takes 1 to 2 months after signing the contract, PHS serves as a direct leading indicator for Existing-Home Sales (Lesson 43). Beginners often misread an index level of 84 as meaning 840,000 homes were sold or that home prices are 16% below 2001 levels.

发布或统计机构
National Association of Realtors NAR
频率与时间
每月 / Monthly · 通常10:00美国东部时间；日期查NAR官网。

这项数据从哪里来？

指数以2001年平均签约活动为100，官方方法说明2001年为最早分析年份；不要把基期误写成首次对公众发布的日期。

The index uses 2001 average contract activity as 100; the methodology identifies that as the first analyzed year, not necessarily its public launch.

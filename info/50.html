NAHB房产市场指数由全美住宅建筑商协会（NAHB）按月发布，通过问卷调查全美独栋住宅建筑商对“当前新房销售状况”、“未来六个月新房销售预期”以及“潜在买家看房客流（Traffic）”三个维度的景气评价。它比月末或下月才发布的新屋开工（第41课）和新屋销售（第44课）时间更早，是观察新房开发商一线体感的最快月度风向标。新手最常犯的错误是把读数55误读成“55%的新房已经卖掉”或“房价要涨55%”，或忘了它只调查独栋住宅建筑商。

English introduction

The NAHB/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index (HMI), published monthly (typically mid-month) by the National Association of Home Builders, surveys U.S. single-family homebuilders on their ratings of current new-home sales, sales expectations over the next six months, and traffic of prospective buyers. Because it releases in the middle of the reference month—weeks ahead of that month's Housing Starts (Lesson 41) and New Home Sales (Lesson 44)—it serves as the earliest monthly sentiment gauge from the homebuilding front lines. Beginners often misread a score of 55 as meaning 55% of homes sold or a 55% price gain, or forget that it surveys only single-family builders.

HMI首次调查于1985年1月，首期读数50；NAHB对独栋建筑商的调查逐渐成为住房景气观察工具。

The inaugural HMI survey was January 1985, with a reading of 50; it surveys single-family builders.

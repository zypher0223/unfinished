English introduction

New orders for manufactured goods generally expected to last at least three years, including machinery, vehicles and aircraft.

发布或统计机构
美国人口普查局 Census
频率与时间
每月 · 耐用品先行报告通常次月下旬东部时间8:30，之后工厂订单报告再修订。

这项数据从哪里来？

美国人口普查局自1957年开展月度M3调查。今天耐用品订单和非国防资本品剔除飞机都是这套调查的统计分组；本课未把M3起始年当成每个现代分项的独立首发年。

Census has conducted the monthly M3 survey since 1957. Durable goods and nondefense capital goods excluding aircraft are groupings within that system; each modern component need not share the survey’s initial release date.

国债净发行规模（Net Marketable Treasury Borrowing）衡量美国财政部在特定期间内通过发行可流通国债向私人市场新筹集的资金总额，减去同期归还到期旧债本金后的净新增借款额。市场每年看到的数十万亿美元“总发行量（Gross Issuance）”中绝大部分其实是在借新还旧（Rollover），只有净借款才是投资者需要额外消化的新增供给。

English introduction

Net Marketable Treasury Borrowing measures the net new funding the U.S. Treasury raises from the private market over a given period by taking gross issuance of marketable Treasury securities and subtracting the principal of maturing securities repaid during the same window. While gross Treasury issuance runs into tens of trillions of dollars annually, the vast majority simply refinances (rolls over) maturing debt; only net borrowing represents incremental new supply that investors must absorb.

发布或统计机构
U.S. Department of the Treasury
频率与时间
季度计划及月/日实际 / Quarterly plans and periodic actuals · 季度再融资文件分次发布；计划与结算后的实际净发行不同。

这项数据从哪里来？

美国政府长期借债融资，当前季度再融资沟通制度逐步形成。此项是发行减到期偿还的会计口径，未核实统一的首次发布日，也无单一交易创始人。

Net borrowing is an accounting measure within Treasury’s evolving financing process; no single trader created it and no unverified first release date is asserted.

English written lesson

Think of net Treasury borrowing like rolling over a monthly credit card balance: if you charge $100 in new purchases while paying off $80 of maturing old balances, your net additional borrowing from the lender is $20, not the $100 gross swipe volume.

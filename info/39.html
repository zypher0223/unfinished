English introduction

G.19 measures consumer credit, including revolving credit cards and nonrevolving auto or student loans, generally excluding real-estate-secured debt.

这项数据从哪里来？

G.19来源于美联储长期消费信贷统计；官方档案记载1942–1952年间有三种相关前身统计。本页不把前身发布日等同于现行G.19首次日期。

G.19 evolved from longstanding Federal Reserve credit statistics; official archives describe three predecessor releases in 1942–1952. That is not necessarily the first date of the present release.

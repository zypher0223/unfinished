加密ETF每日资金流量（Crypto ETF Daily Net Flows）统计现货比特币或以太坊等交易所交易基金（ETF）在每个美股交易日内，因基金份额净申购（Net Creations）与净赎回（Net Redemptions）所对应的资金净流入或净流出金额。它衡量的是通过合规证券基金这一特定渠道进入或撤出底层加密资产的边际净需求，既不等于全球加密交易所的24小时现货与合约买卖成交总额，。

English introduction

Crypto ETF Daily Net Flows track the net dollar amount of capital entering or leaving spot cryptocurrency exchange-traded funds (such as spot Bitcoin or Ether ETFs) on each U.S. equity trading day through primary-market share creations minus redemptions. This metric gauges marginal net demand flowing through regulated brokerage and fund channels; it does not represent total 24-hour spot and derivatives trading volume across global crypto exchanges, nor does it capture every institutional investor's net directional exposure.

发布或统计机构
ETF发行商；供应商汇总如Farside
频率与时间
交易日每日 / Each trading day · 发行商更新净值、份额及持仓后，供应商汇总；完整表常晚于美股收盘。

这项数据从哪里来？

美国现货比特币ETP于2024年1月获批并上市，使每日净申赎更受关注；ETF和海外加密基金更早就存在，不能说2024年才首次有ETF。

U.S. spot bitcoin ETP approvals and launches in January 2024 made daily flows prominent; ETFs and overseas crypto products predate them.

English written lesson

Think of daily ETF net flows as counting how many new warehouse receipts a bullion vault issues or cancels at the end of the day (which requires adding or removing physical bars in the vault); when investors simply trade existing receipts back and forth on the stock exchange during the day, that secondary trading volume changes ownership without automatically forcing the vault to buy or sell underlying coins.

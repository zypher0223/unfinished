English introduction

New applications for unemployment insurance during a week, a timely indicator of layoff pressure but not a count of all newly unemployed people.

发布或统计机构

频率与时间

这项数据从哪里来？

失业保险统计来自各州申报管理系统，由美国劳工部汇总。它与BLS抽样失业率不同；本课未核实今日季调周度初请或续请精确版本的首次发布日期，不用最早可下载观测值冒充首发历史。

Claims statistics arise from state unemployment-insurance administration and are compiled by DOL, unlike the BLS survey unemployment rate. The first publication of today’s exact seasonally adjusted weekly series has not been verified here.

个人储蓄率（Personal Saving Rate）由美国经济分析局（BEA）按月随《个人收入与支出》报告发布，用来衡量美国整个居民部门当期税后可支配收入（DPI）在扣除个人消费支出、非房贷利息支出和经常性转移支付之后，剩余部分占可支配收入的百分比。它反映的是全国居民当月新增收支的结余比例，而不是统计普通家庭银行活期账户里的累计存款余额。新手最常把它误读成“银行存款同比增速”，。

English introduction

The Personal Saving Rate, published monthly by the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) within the Personal Income and Outlays report, measures the percentage of disposable personal income (after-tax income) remaining after households pay for personal consumption expenditures, non-mortgage interest, and current transfer payments. It captures the monthly flow ratio of unspent income across the entire U.S. household sector rather than cumulative cash sitting in bank accounts. Beginners often misread it as bank deposit growth or assume stock and home price appreciation boosts the saving rate.

美国经济分析局 BEA

每月 / Monthly · 随 Personal Income and Outlays 发布；具体日期查 BEA 日历。

这项数据从哪里来？

储蓄率来自美国国民收入与产品账户，不是某位交易员发明的指标。现行月度历史序列可回溯到1959年；样本起点不等于首次公布日。本页不把尚未核实的首次发布日写成确定事实。

The measure is part of the national income and product accounts, not a trader invention. The monthly series extends to 1959; a historical start is not the first publication date.

中文文字教学

像全国居民部门想成一个大家庭：这个月发到手税后工资100元，衣食住行及信用卡利息一共花掉96元，当月剩下4元没花，储蓄率就是4%。但这个比喻的边界在于：家庭拿这4元去买股票或存银行都属于储蓄去向，而手里老股票涨价赚的钱在国民账户里属于资本利得，。

English written lesson

Think of the household sector as one big family earning $100 after taxes and spending $96 on living expenses and interest, leaving $4 unspent for a 4% saving rate; the analogy breaks down when investments rise in value, because unrealized capital gains on stocks or homes are excluded from current disposable income.

M2货币供应量（M2 Money Stock）是美联储统计的广义流动性指标，汇总了实体经济中可直接用于支付的货币以及较容易转化为支付手段的近似货币资产。美国M2既不代表全球离岸美元的总规模，也不等于美联储总资产或商业银行的准备金余额。

English introduction

M2 Money Stock is a broad monetary aggregate published by the Federal Reserve that sums transaction money immediately available for spending (M1, including currency outside banks, demand deposits, and savings deposits) together with near-money assets easily convertible into cash (small-denomination time deposits under $100,000 and retail money market fund shares). Importantly, U.S. M2 does not capture all offshore Eurodollars worldwide, nor is it identical to total Federal Reserve assets or commercial bank reserve balances.

发布或统计机构
Federal Reserve Board
频率与时间
每月 / Monthly · H.6按官方日历通常周二13:00美国东部时间。

这项数据从哪里来？

现代M2来自美联储货币总量统计，定义经历多次调整；H.6发布前身可追溯1960年。不能把某次定义变更当作货币首次出现。

Modern M2 evolved through changing Fed monetary definitions; H.6 predecessor releases date to 1960, not the origin of money.

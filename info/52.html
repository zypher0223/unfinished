这是商业银行等存在美联储的账户余额，主要用于银行间支付清算。不是普通居民储蓄，也不是银行保险箱里全部现金。

English introduction

These are balances banks hold at Federal Reserve Banks for settlement, not household deposits or all vault cash.

Reserves arise from the central bank settlement system. Interest on reserves began in 2008 and the ample-reserves framework changed implementation.

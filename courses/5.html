VWMA（成交量加权移动平均线，在最近固定N根K线窗口内按每根K线成交量大小赋予权重求平均的均线）回答：如果让成交量大的K线在平均价里说话更有分量、成交量小的K线少占分量，最近N根的平均成本在哪里？它直接画在主图K线上，且每根K线向前滚动更新，不同于每天从开盘重置的VWAP。新手常把单家交易所的VWMA当成全市场总成本。

English introduction

VWMA (Volume Weighted Moving Average, a rolling N-bar average that weights each closing price in proportion to that bar's trading volume) asks where the N-bar average price sits when high-volume bars carry more weight than quiet bars. It plots on the main price chart over a rolling window, unlike session VWAP which resets at the start of each session. Beginners often confuse VWMA with VWAP or treat one feed's volume as the entire market.

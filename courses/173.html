很多亏损不是因为不会看图，而是因为人很自然的反应：怕亏、急着落袋、觉得“涨这么多该跌了”。这些反应不能消除，但可以在交易前写好规则，交易后用记录对照，看自己在哪种偏差上反复犯错。

English introduction

Many losses come not from misreading charts but from perfectly human reactions: fear of losing, rushing to bank gains, feeling that “after such a rise it must fall.” You cannot remove these reactions, but you can write rules before trading and check your records afterwards to see which bias keeps catching you.

提出者与来源

行为金融学：Kahneman 与 Tversky、Shefrin 与 Statman 等 — Kahneman 与 Tversky 在 1979 年提出前景理论，描述了损失厌恶；他们还用“代表性启发”解释赌徒谬误。Hersh Shefrin 与 Meir Statman 在 1985 年的论文中提出“处置效应”一词。习思cis在书中用自己的亏损经历讲到胜负守恒的错觉、急于锁盈与以过去价格判断贵贱。

Kahneman and Tversky’s 1979 prospect theory described loss aversion, and they explained the gambler’s fallacy through the representativeness heuristic. Hersh Shefrin and Meir Statman coined the term “disposition effect” in a 1985 paper. cis draws on his own losses to describe the illusion that wins and losses must even out, the rush to lock in gains, and judging price against the past.

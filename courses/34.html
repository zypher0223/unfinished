English introduction

OBV (On Balance Volume, a cumulative line that adds the entire bar's volume when Close exceeds prior Close and subtracts the entire bar's volume when Close is below prior Close) asks whether volume on up-close bars has outpaced volume on down-close bars over time. It plots as a running total in a lower pane. Beginners often fixate on the raw axis number or assume OBV measures actual cash inflows.

中文文字教学

它像一种“赢家通吃”的记分规则：只要今天收盘比昨天高哪怕1分钱，今天全场所有观众人数都加给红队；只要比昨天低1分钱，全场人数都扣掉；因此总分从哪天开始算并不重要，重要的是积分曲线是往上升还是往下降。

English written lesson

It uses a winner-take-all scoring rule: if today's close beats yesterday's by even a single cent, 100% of today's attendance is added to the score, and if it finishes one cent lower, 100% is subtracted; thus only the slope and peaks matter, not the starting total.

美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）的入门材料说明：多数期货合约在交割日前就被了结，有些合约用现金结算代替实物交割；客户账户每天按收盘时的市值调整。也就是说，期货的盈亏不是等到平仓才算，而是每个交易日收盘都结算一次，亏损当天就从账户扣掉，保证金不够要补。期货只需交一小部分保证金就能持有整张合约的名义价值，所以杠杆很高。CME Group 的教程写明：合约的到期日是能交易它的最后一天；移仓时交易者常看两个月份的成交量，在下个月份成交量上来后，同时平掉当前合约并开立下一个月份的合约。加密货币里的永续合约（第 182、108 课）没有到期日，用资金费率代替移仓。

English introduction

The US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) explains that most futures contracts are liquidated before the delivery date, that some allow cash settlement instead of delivery, and that customer accounts are adjusted to each trading day’s market value at the close. In other words, futures gains and losses are settled every trading day, not when you close: losses come out of the account that day, and if margin falls short you must top it up. A small margin deposit controls the contract’s full notional value, so leverage is high. CME Group’s course notes that the expiration date is the last day the contract can trade, and that traders often watch volume in both months, rolling once volume builds in the next month by closing the current contract and opening the next at the same time. Crypto perpetual futures (lessons 182 and 108) have no expiry and use funding rates instead of rolling.

提出者与来源

本课的平仓、移仓与交割说明依据 CFTC 和 CME Group 的投资者教育材料 — CFTC《期货交易基础》说明多数合约在交割日前了结、部分合约现金结算、账户按每日收盘市值调整；CME Group 教程说明到期前的三种选择：平仓、移仓、进入交割（实物或现金，视市场而定）。

The CFTC’s “Basics of Futures Trading” says most contracts are liquidated before delivery, some settle in cash, and accounts are adjusted to each day’s closing value; CME Group’s course lists three choices before expiry: offset, roll, or go to settlement (physical or cash, depending on the market).

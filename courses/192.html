Seasonality & Calendar Effects 季节性与日历效应

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

市场常流传“一月效应”“五月卖出”等日历口诀。部分源于税务或调仓制度，更多只是数据巧合。当规律广为人知后，抢跑资金往往会让效应减弱甚至失效。

English introduction

Market folklore is filled with calendar adages like the 'January Effect' or 'Sell in May and Go Away'. While some stem from tax harvesting, institutional rebalancing, or agricultural cycles, many are data-mined flukes that fade once widely publicized.

提出者与来源

西德尼·瓦赫特尔与金融异象学术研究 — 西德尼·瓦赫特尔（Sidney B. Wachtel）1942年在《Journal of Business》发表《Certain Observations on Seasonal Movements in Stock Prices》，较早记录了股市年初季节性上涨现象（后称“一月效应”）；唐纳德·凯姆（Donald Keim，1983）发现一月效应主要集中在小盘股，斯文·鲍曼与本·雅各布森（Sven Bouman & Ben Jacobsen，2002）则系统研究了“五月卖出”（万圣节指标）。

Sidney B. Wachtel's 1942 Journal of Business article 'Certain Observations on Seasonal Movements in Stock Prices' was an early record of the year-start seasonal rise later called the January Effect; Donald Keim (1983) showed it was concentrated in small caps, and Sven Bouman & Ben Jacobsen (2002) studied 'Sell in May' (the Halloween indicator).

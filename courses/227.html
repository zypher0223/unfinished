Concentrated Liquidity & Liquidity Management 集中流动性与流动性管理

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

早期自动做市商（如 Uniswap v2）将做市商（LP）存入的资产均匀铺在 0 到无穷大的全价格区间（见本站「DEXs & AMMs 去中心化交易所与自动做市商」一课），绝大部分资金闲置在远离市价的极端位置。集中流动性（如 Uniswap v3）允许 LP 设定自定义价格区间（Ticks）：只要市价停留在区间内，就能用更少本金提供同等盘口深度并获取数倍手续费分成，每个区间头寸以非同质化代币（ERC-721 正式标准 NFT）表示。但高资金效率伴随更高风险：一旦市价跌破下限或突破上限，头寸就会完全变成单边贬值或踏空的单一资产，且暂停赚取手续费；区间越窄，单位本金承受的无常损失（Impermanent Loss）越显著。若频繁手动撤池移仓（Rebalancing），不仅承担兑换滑点与燃料费，还易在震荡市中陷入“低点割肉、高点追回”的本金磨损。

English introduction

In early automated market makers like Uniswap v2, liquidity providers (LPs) spread their capital uniformly across the entire price curve from zero to infinity (see the "DEXs & AMMs" lesson on this site), leaving most capital idle far from the current market price. Concentrated liquidity (introduced in Uniswap v3) allows LPs to allocate capital within custom price ranges defined by discrete ticks (for example, providing ETH/USDC liquidity strictly between $1,800 and $2,200). As long as the market price stays inside that active range, an LP can provide the same market depth and earn the same fee share with a fraction of the capital, with each custom position represented as a unique ERC-721 NFT. However, higher capital efficiency comes with sharp trade-offs: the moment the market price moves below the lower bound or above the upper bound, the position converts 100% into the less valuable asset, stops earning swap fees entirely, and experiences amplified Impermanent Loss per dollar deployed. Furthermore, actively rebalancing ranges incurs swap slippage, gas fees, and the risk of locking in permanent losses during whipsaw volatility.

提出者与来源

2021 年 5 月 Uniswap v3 引入集中流动性（Ticks）与 2025 年 v4 钩子（Hooks）演进 — 2021 年 3 月 Uniswap 团队发布 v3 白皮书并于同年 5 月上线以太坊主网，首次将离散价格刻度（Ticks）与集中流动性引入自动做市商，使每份 LP 头寸因区间不同而成为非同质化的 ERC-721 NFT（EIP-721 为以太坊正式标准）。2025 年 1 月上线的 Uniswap v4 在保留集中流动性机制的基础上，进一步引入单例池架构（Singleton）与可定制钩子合约（Hooks），降低了建池与多跳兑换的燃料费消耗。

Uniswap Labs published the v3 whitepaper in March 2021 and launched on Ethereum mainnet in May 2021, introducing discrete price ticks and concentrated liquidity represented by ERC-721 NFTs (an official Final Ethereum standard). In January 2025, Uniswap v4 retained concentrated liquidity while introducing a singleton contract architecture and customizable Hooks.

English written lesson

Full-range liquidity is like setting up water stands every mile along a 500-mile highway where most stands sit empty; concentrated liquidity packs all your inventory right at the busiest rest stop—earning far more sales per dollar of stock, until traffic detours onto another road (price leaves your range), leaving your stand with zero customers unless you pay to haul everything to the new route.

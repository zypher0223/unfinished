Correlation Coefficient（相关系数，衡量两个品种在一段时间内同步涨跌程度的统计指标）回答：最近N根K线里，品种A和品种B是经常同向移动、反向移动，还是各走各的？它在副图-1到+1之间波动。新手常误以为相关系数+0.8就代表“A涨1%时B也必定涨1%”，或者把短期同步当成永远不变的因果铁律。

English introduction

The Correlation Coefficient (a statistical measure of how closely two assets move together over a rolling window) asks whether Asset A and Asset B have moved in the same direction, opposite directions, or independently over the last N bars. It plots between -1 and +1 in a lower pane. Beginners often assume +0.8 means equal percentage gains or permanent causation.

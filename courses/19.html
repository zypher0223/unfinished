MFI（资金流量指数，Money Flow Index，把每根K线的典型价格乘以成交量算作原始资金流、再按典型价涨跌分成正向与负向资金流代入RSI公式的指标）回答：把价格高低和成交量大小绑在一起算，最近14根K线里伴随上涨的量价总和与伴随下跌的量价总和谁占优？它在副图0到100之间画单线。新手常误以为MFI=80代表“有80%的场外真金白银净流入”。

English introduction

MFI (Money Flow Index, a volume-weighted cousin of RSI that multiplies each bar's typical price by volume and compares sums on up-TP bars versus down-TP bars) asks how volume-weighted upward bars compare with volume-weighted downward bars over a 14-bar window. It plots a single line from 0 to 100 in a lower pane. Beginners often assume MFI at 80 means 80% of all money flowed into the asset.

中文文字教学

如果普通RSI是只按每局拔河移动了几米来记分，MFI就是把每局移动的距离再乘上那局上场的人数（成交量）来记分；但场上有人买就必有人卖，它只按“今天均价比昨天高还是低”把全天成交量一刀切归给多方或空方。

English written lesson

Whereas standard RSI scores only price distance, MFI weights each bar by how many participants traded (volume); however, it assigns 100% of a bar's volume to the positive bucket whenever typical price ticks up by even one cent.

MA Cross回答：最近几天的短期平均价，是否已经高过或低于过去几周的较长期平均价？它直接画在主图K线上，交点处常标有小十字或箭头。新手最容易迷信“金叉必涨、死叉必跌”的口诀。

English introduction

MA Cross (Moving Average Crossover, an overlay plotting a shorter, faster moving average alongside a longer, slower moving average) asks whether the recent short-term average price has moved above or below the longer-term average price. It plots both lines and crossover markers directly on the main price chart. Beginners often treat 'Golden Cross' and 'Death Cross' labels as assured profit signals.

提出者与来源

无公认唯一发明人；Richard Donchian 为有据可查的早期使用者 / No agreed inventor; Donchian a documented early user — 均线交叉没有公认的唯一发明人。有据可查的早期使用者之一是 Richard Donchian：他自1960年起在 Hayden Stone 主编周报《Commodity Trend Timing》，采用5日/20日均线交叉法；但这不能证明他是最早提出者。

Moving-average crossing has no agreed single inventor. A well-documented early practitioner is Richard Donchian, whose weekly Commodity Trend Timing letter (from 1960 at Hayden Stone) used a 5/20-day crossover; this does not prove he was first.

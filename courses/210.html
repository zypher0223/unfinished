同样是 40% 的隐含波动率，放在常年平静的蓝筹股上算极贵，放在高波动科技股或加密货币上却可能算便宜。为了判断某标的当前的期权究竟处于自身历史的偏贵还是偏便宜水位（区别于过去真实波动，见本站「Historical Volatility 历史波动率」及「Options Basics 期权入门」一课），交易者使用 IV Rank 与 IV Percentile 进行纵向对比，并重点防范财报落地后的波动率压缩（见本站「Earnings Season & Guidance 财报季与业绩预期」一课）。

English introduction

An Implied Volatility reading of 40% might be historically expensive for a quiet utility stock yet historically cheap for a high-beta tech stock or cryptocurrency. To judge whether an asset's options are rich or cheap relative to its own past (distinct from realized past movement—see the "Historical Volatility" and "Options Basics" lessons on this site), traders compare IV Rank and IV Percentile, while watching out for post-event Volatility Crush around catalysts (see the "Earnings Season & Guidance" lesson on this site).

提出者与来源

隐含波动率概念源于 1976 年 Latané 与 Rendleman 的研究；Cboe 于 1993 年推出 VIX 指数 — 1976 年经济学家 Henry Latané 与 Richard Rendleman 发表论文提出从 Black–Scholes 模型反推隐含标准差；1993 年芝加哥期权交易所（Cboe）基于罗伯特·威利（Robert Whaley）的设计推出 VIX 波动率指数（见本站「VIX 波动率指数」一课）；近年，零售期权平台普及了 IV Rank 与 IV Percentile 指标以辅助散户横向比较波动率高低。

In 1976, economists Henry Latané and Richard J. Rendleman introduced implied standard deviations backed out from the Black–Scholes formula. In 1993, Cboe launched the VIX index designed by Professor Robert Whaley (see the "VIX Volatility Index" lesson on this site), and in recent years retail options platforms popularized IV Rank and IV Percentile.

中文文字教学

IV 好比台风来临前海鲜市场的实时菜价；IV Rank 是看今天菜价处于全年最高价与最低价标尺的几分之几位置；IV Percentile 是数过去一年里有几天菜价比今天便宜；而波动率压缩（IV Crush）就像台风警报解除的清晨，恐慌囤货消失，菜价瞬间跳水回落。

English written lesson

Think of IV as the live price of umbrellas before a storm; IV Rank marks where today's price sits on the ruler between the year's cheapest and most expensive price tags; IV Percentile counts how many days over the past year umbrellas cost less than today; and IV Crush is the morning the storm warning is canceled, when panic demand vanishes and prices drop instantly.

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

很多人以为买 NFT 就是买下一张图片。实际上，链上记录的通常只是代币编号和持有人；图片可能存在区块链上，也可能存在 IPFS（一种按内容寻址的分布式存储）或项目方自己的服务器上，服务器停了，图片可能打不开或被替换。能不能拿去商用，要看项目写的授权条款。版税（每次转手时付给创作者的一部分）有 ERC-2981 标准，它同样是 Final，但它只规定怎样查询版税信息，标准原文写明付款是自愿的，交易市场可以不执行。另外，“地板价”是挂单里最低的卖价，不是你能卖出的价格；买单（出价）稀少时，想卖出要接受更低的价格。

English introduction

Many people think buying an NFT means buying an image. In fact the chain usually records only the token ID and its holder; the image may be on-chain, on IPFS (distributed storage addressed by content) or on the project’s own server, and if that server stops, the image may fail to load or be swapped. Whether you may use it commercially depends on the licence the project publishes. Royalties (a share paid to the creator on each resale) have a standard, ERC-2981, which is also Final, but it only defines how to look up royalty information, and its text states that payment is voluntary, so marketplaces may not enforce it. Also, the “floor price” is the lowest asking price among listings, not a price you can sell at; when bids are thin, selling means accepting less.

提出者与来源

ERC-721 于 2018 年提出，ERC-1155 同年提出，ERC-2981 于 2020 年提出 — 这三个标准在以太坊官方 EIP 仓库都已定稿（Final）。ERC-721 定义了逐个编号的代币接口；ERC-1155 让一个合约同时管理多种代币；ERC-2981 只定义查询版税的方法，不强制付款。

All three are Final in the official Ethereum EIP repository. ERC-721 defines an interface for individually numbered tokens; ERC-1155 lets one contract manage many token types; ERC-2981 only defines how to query royalties and does not force payment.

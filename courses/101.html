Volume Delta回答：在当前这一根K线的时间里，按分类规则算出的买方成交量比卖方成交量多多少或少多少？它在副图围绕0线画柱子或K线。新手最容易犯的误解，是看到Delta=+200就以为“场内只有买盘没有卖盘”，或者不知道许多看盘软件只是拿1分钟小K线涨跌去估算Delta。

English introduction

Volume Delta (an indicator subtracting classified sell-side volume from classified buy-side volume within each candle) asks whether buyer-classified or seller-classified volume was larger inside a single bar. It plots bars or candles around a zero line in a lower pane. Beginners often assume Delta of +200 means '600 buyers and only 400 sellers existed', or overlook that many platforms estimate Delta from lower-timeframe bars rather than tick data.

提出者与来源

订单流统计概念；单一创始人未确认 / Order-flow measure — 买卖成交差值是通用统计；TradingView具体内置算法由其平台实现，未证实概念的首位提出者。

Buy/sell volume difference is a general statistical concept. TradingView supplies a particular implementation; the concept's first inventor is unverified.

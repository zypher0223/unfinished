Volume Delta 成交量差值

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

它比较一根K线内偏买方与偏卖方的成交量差额。注意 TradingView 内置版本主要用更小周期价格变化估算方向。

English introduction

Volume Delta compares estimated buy-side and sell-side volume within a candle. TradingView's built-in version mainly estimates direction from lower-timeframe price changes.

提出者与来源

订单流统计概念；单一创始人未确认 / Order-flow measure — 买卖成交差值是通用统计；TradingView具体内置算法由其平台实现，未证实概念的首位提出者。

Buy/sell volume difference is a general statistical concept. TradingView supplies a particular implementation; the concept's first inventor is unverified.

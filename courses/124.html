ICT 交易方法

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

这是一套课程体系，常把交易时段、前高前低、结构变化与FVG等区域组合。FVG常指三根K线中第一根与第三根影线之间留下的不重叠价格带。

English introduction

ICT is a course framework combining session timing, prior highs/lows, structure and areas such as fair value gaps. A common FVG definition is non-overlap between the wicks of the first and third candles in a three-candle sequence.

提出者与来源

Michael J. Huddleston（The Inner Circle Trader） — ICT 是 Huddleston 使用的教学品牌；归属其课程框架不表示所有底层趋势、供需或时段概念都由他首次发明。

ICT is Huddleston's teaching identity and framework. This does not mean he invented every underlying trend, supply/demand or session concept.

HMA 赫尔均线

HMA（赫尔移动平均线，通过把两倍半周期WMA减去全周期WMA、再对结果做√N周期WMA平滑的低滞后均线）回答：怎样通过数学加权抵消一部分均线落后于价格的距离，让曲线既保持光滑又能更快拐弯？它直接画在主图K线上。新手最常犯的错误是看它紧贴价格且拐弯快，就以为它能提前预测拐点、甚至见拐头就反向重仓。

English introduction

HMA (Hull Moving Average, a low-lag average that subtracts a full-length WMA from twice a half-length WMA and smooths the result with a √N-length WMA) asks how to keep a moving average smooth while reducing the trailing lag behind price. It plots directly on the main price chart. Beginners often mistake its tight fit and fast turns for predictive foresight.

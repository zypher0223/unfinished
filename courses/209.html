Greeks II: Theta & Vega 希腊字母二：Theta 与 Vega

为什么有时股价方向看对了，买入的期权却依然亏钱？因为在标的价格（Delta/Gamma）之外，时间流逝与市场对未来波动率的预期时刻在重塑期权的时间价值（见本站「Options Basics 期权入门」一课）。Theta 刻画了每天被日历拿走的租金，而 Vega 则刻画了市场恐慌或平静情绪对期权定价的放大与压缩。期权买方与卖方在这两个维度上始终处于镜像博弈的位置。

English introduction

Why can an option buyer guess the stock's direction correctly and still lose money? Beyond underlying price moves (Delta and Gamma), the passage of time and shifts in expected volatility constantly reshape an option's time value (see the "Options Basics" lesson on this site). Theta measures the daily rent eroded by the calendar, while Vega measures how rising or falling implied volatility inflates or deflates option premiums. Buyers and sellers sit on opposite sides of both forces.

提出者与来源

Theta 来自 Black–Scholes–Merton 框架的时间敏感度；“Vega”不是希腊字母，是业界沿用的约定叫法 — 在 Black–Scholes–Merton 框架中，期权价格对时间的偏导数称为 Theta（Θ）。期权价格对波动率（σ）的敏感度在业界被叫作“Vega”——它并不是希腊字母表里的字母，通常解释为借用了 Volatility 的首字母 V，有时用符号 ν 表示。

In the Black–Scholes–Merton framework, the partial derivative of option value with respect to time is called Theta (Θ). Sensitivity to volatility (σ) is called "Vega" by practitioners; it is not a letter of the Greek alphabet and is commonly explained as borrowing the V of Volatility, sometimes written with the symbol ν.

English written lesson

Think of an option's time value as an ice cube sitting outdoors: Theta is the steady drip of daily melting that speeds up as the deadline nears, while Vega is the outside weather—rising implied volatility refreezes and thickens the ice, whereas falling implied volatility is a sudden heatwave that shrinks it rapidly.

Bollinger BandWidth回答：当前布林带上下轨张开的口子，相当于中轨价格的百分之几？它把主图布林带的宽窄单独提炼成副图里的一根单线。新手最容易犯的误解，是看到带宽降到极低位置（挤压）就以为价格接下来通常会向上大涨。

English introduction

Bollinger BandWidth (an indicator dividing the distance between the upper and lower Bollinger Bands by the middle band) asks how wide the bands are as a percentage of the middle average. It plots a single line from zero upward in a lower pane. Beginners often assume an unusually low BandWidth reading ensures an upward rally, forgetting that volatility expansion is completely directionless.

提出者与来源

John Bollinger — Bollinger本人官网说明，他在布林带之后增加了BandWidth；这里不采用互相冲突的二手首次年份，也不声称核实了首笔实盘。

Bollinger states on his website that he added BandWidth after the bands. No disputed secondary first-year claim or first live trade is asserted here.

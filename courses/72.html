Pring Special K（普林格Special K，把从短到极长的十几组平滑价格变动率全部加总在一起的超长周期动量指标）回答：如果把短线、中线和长达一两年的长线涨跌速度统统合在一根线上，当前市场的大方向处于上升期还是下降期？它在副图围绕0线画出主线和信号线。新手常误拿它去做几分钟的超短线追涨杀跌。

English introduction

Pring's Special K (a composite momentum indicator summing a dozen smoothed rates of change from short to very long lookbacks) asks which way the primary cycle points when short, intermediate, and multi-hundred-bar momentum are combined into one line. It plots a main line and a double-smoothed signal line in a lower pane. Beginners often misuse it as a fast short-term timing trigger.

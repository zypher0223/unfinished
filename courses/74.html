Coppock Curve（科波克曲线，把14周期与11周期的价格变动率相加后再做10周期加权移动平均的长期动量指标）回答：在经历漫长的下跌低迷期之后，长期的涨跌动力是否开始企稳回暖？它在副图围绕0线画出一条平滑曲线。新手最常犯的错误是把它直接搬到1分钟或5分钟图上当成超短线抓买卖点的工具。

English introduction

The Coppock Curve (a long-term momentum indicator applying a 10-period weighted moving average to the sum of 14-period and 11-period rates of change) asks whether long-term downside momentum is beginning to ease after a prolonged market decline. It plots a single smooth curve around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often misuse it on 1-minute charts without understanding its monthly design.

提出者与来源

Edwin S. Coppock — 官方记载 Coppock 于 1962 年在 Barron's 发表公式。

Official documentation records Coppock's 1962 Barron's publication.

CMF（蔡金资金流，Chaikin Money Flow，把最近N根K线的收盘位置加权成交量相加后再除以同期总成交量的指标）回答：在最近20根左右的K线窗口里，按收盘位置加权的成交量净占比是偏正还是偏负？它在副图-1到+1之间围绕0线摆动。新手最容易犯的误解是把CMF=0.15当成“有15%的场外资金净流入”。

English introduction

CMF (Chaikin Money Flow, an oscillator dividing the N-bar sum of Money Flow Volume by the N-bar sum of total volume) asks whether volume over a rolling 20-bar window has favored upper-range closes or lower-range closes. It oscillates between -1 and +1 around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often misread +0.15 as meaning 15% net cash inflow into the market.

English written lesson

Whereas ADL is a lifetime running ledger, CMF scores only the last 20 days' net weighted volume as a share of those 20 days' total volume; however, when an old high-volume day drops out of the 20-day window, the ratio can jump even if today is quiet.

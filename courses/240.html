Straddles & Strangles 跨式与宽跨：押注大波动而不是方向

我的学习与错题新手路线

跨式和宽跨常被用在财报、央行决议这类可能引发大波动的事件前。按 OIC 的说明，跨式的两个盈亏平衡点是“行权价 ± 两份权利金之和”；宽跨是“看涨行权价 + 权利金之和”和“看跌行权价 − 权利金之和”。时间流逝对它们很不利（两张期权的时间价值同时流失），隐含波动率上升则有利。问题在于：事件前大家都预期会有大波动，隐含波动率通常已经很高，权利金很贵；公布之后隐含波动率往往大幅回落（第 210 课的波动率压缩），价格动得不够大，两张期权一起贬值。宽跨比跨式便宜，但需要更大的价格变动才能回本。

English introduction

Straddles and strangles are often used before events that might cause big moves, such as earnings or central-bank decisions. Per OIC, a straddle’s two breakevens are the strike ± the total premium; a strangle’s are the call strike + total premium and the put strike − total premium. Time decay hurts them badly (both options lose time value at once), while rising implied volatility helps. The catch: before the event everyone expects a big move, so implied volatility is usually already high and the premiums expensive; after the announcement implied volatility often drops sharply (the volatility crush in lesson 210), and if the price does not move enough, both options lose value together. A strangle is cheaper than a straddle but needs a bigger move to break even.

提出者与来源

跨式与宽跨是标准期权组合，没有单一发明人；本课盈亏平衡点算式与 OIC 的策略说明一致 — OIC 的买入跨式说明写明最大亏损为两份权利金、时间衰减影响“极其重要且为负”、隐含波动率上升有利；买入宽跨（又称买入组合）说明给出看涨行权价加权利金、看跌行权价减权利金两个盈亏平衡点。

OIC’s long-straddle notes give a maximum loss of both premiums, call time decay “extremely important, negative effect”, and say rising implied volatility helps; its long-strangle (long combination) notes give breakevens at the call strike plus premiums and the put strike minus premiums.

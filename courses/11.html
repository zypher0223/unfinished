RSI 相对强弱指数

我的学习与错题新手路线

RSI（相对强弱指数，比较最近一段窗口内上涨日平均涨幅与下跌日平均跌幅之比的动量指标）回答：在最近14根K线里，向上涨的力道和往下跌的力道放在一起比，哪一边占了上风？它在副图0到100之间画出一根单线。新手最容易误解名字里的“相对强弱”，以为它是拿两只股票比谁更强，或者把70分当成“价格贵了70%”。

English introduction

RSI (Relative Strength Index, a momentum oscillator comparing smoothed average gains against smoothed average losses over N bars) asks whether upward or downward bar-to-bar changes have been stronger over the recent window. It plots a single line from 0 to 100 in a lower pane. Beginners often think 'Relative Strength' compares two different assets or assume a reading of 75 means a 75% chance of a drop.

提出者与来源

J. Welles Wilder Jr. — Wilder在1978年《New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems》中介绍RSI；不据此推断其首笔实盘日期。

Wilder introduced RSI in his 1978 book New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems. His first live trade using it is not established here.

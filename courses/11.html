RSI 相对强弱指数

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

RSI把最近上涨和下跌的力度压成0到100的分数。数值高说明最近涨得更强，低说明最近跌得更强；它不比较两种资产。

English introduction

RSI compares recent upward and downward price changes on a 0–100 scale. A high reading means recent upward movement has been stronger; it does not compare two assets.

提出者与来源

J. Welles Wilder Jr. — Wilder在1978年《New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems》中介绍RSI；不据此推断其首笔实盘日期。

Wilder introduced RSI in his 1978 book New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems. His first live trade using it is not established here.

TPO/Market Profile回答：在这段时间里，价格在哪个价位停留的时段个数最多、在哪个价位只是短暂露面就离开？它画在主图上，核心衡量的是“时间停留（Time）”，而不是“成交量（Volume）”。新手最常把TPO字母最多的一行误当成成交量最大的一行。

English introduction

TPO / Market Profile (Time Price Opportunity, a charting method that divides each session into fixed time blocks—typically 30-minute periods labeled A, B, C—and stacks a letter at every price row visited during that block) asks where price spent the most time during a session versus where it visited only briefly. It plots letter profiles on the main chart, measuring time spent at price rather than traded volume. Beginners often confuse the widest row of TPO letters with the highest-volume price.

提出者与来源

J. Peter Steidlmayer；Chicago Board of Trade — Wiley 的作者介绍明确指出 Steidlmayer 在CBOT任职期间推动 Market Profile 与 Liquidity Data Bank；现代实现由多人发展。

Wiley credits Steidlmayer with initiating Market Profile and the Liquidity Data Bank at CBOT. Later implementations have multiple contributors.

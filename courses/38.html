Parabolic SAR回答：如果给当前顺势走势设一条每创一次新高（或新低）就自动追得更紧一点的移动参考线，这条线现在在哪？它直接画在主图K线上下方。新手常以为点翻边时系统会自动帮账户平仓反手。

English introduction

Parabolic SAR (Stop and Reverse, an overlay plotting a trail of dots below or above price that accelerates closer each time a new extreme is reached and flips sides when touched) asks where an accelerating trailing reference sits during an active move. It plots directly on the main price chart. Beginners often assume a dot flip automatically executes a stop order or works reliably in sideways markets.

提出者与来源

J. Welles Wilder Jr. — Wilder在1978年书中介绍Parabolic SAR，SAR意为Stop and Reverse。首笔实盘使用日期未核实。

Wilder introduced Parabolic SAR in his 1978 book. SAR means Stop and Reverse. First live trading use was not verified.

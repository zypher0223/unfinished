Shooting Star & Inverted Hammer 射击之星与倒锤子

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

射击之星和倒锤子也是同形异名：小实体、长上影线。上涨之后，长上影说明冲高被打回，叫射击之星；下跌之后，长上影说明买方试探过，叫倒锤子。两者都要看下一根。

English introduction

The shooting star and inverted hammer are another same-shape pair: a small body with a long upper shadow. After a rise, the long upper shadow shows a rally that was pushed back (shooting star); after a decline, it shows buyers testing higher (inverted hammer). Both need the next bar.

提出者与来源

日本蜡烛图传统；Steve Nison 在西方推广 — 蜡烛图形态源自日本的米市交易传统，具体创始人未确认（常被归于本间宗久，但缺少可靠原始文献）；1991 年 Steve Nison 的《Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques》让它在西方普及。

Candlestick patterns come from Japanese rice-trading tradition; the specific originator is unverified (often attributed to Munehisa Homma without reliable primary sources). Steve Nison’s 1991 “Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques” popularised them in the West.

Rounding Bottoms & V Reversals 圆弧底与 V 形反转

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

圆弧底是慢反转：卖方力量一点点减弱，买方一点点接手，过程长、看得到。V 形反转是快反转：急跌之后马上急涨，事先几乎没有迹象，只能事后识别，追进去的风险也更大。

English introduction

A rounding bottom is a slow reversal: selling fades gradually and buyers take over gradually, over a long and visible process. A V reversal is fast: a sharp drop followed straight away by a sharp rise, with almost no warning, so it can only be recognised afterwards and chasing it is riskier.

提出者与来源

经典图表形态；Richard Schabacker 与 Edwards、Magee 系统整理 — 这类形态在 1930 年代 Richard Schabacker 的著作中已有系统描述，1948 年 Edwards 与 Magee 的《Technical Analysis of Stock Trends》进一步普及；最早的使用者未确认。

Such patterns were described systematically by Richard Schabacker in the 1930s and popularised by Edwards and Magee’s 1948 “Technical Analysis of Stock Trends”; the earliest users are unverified.

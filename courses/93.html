Anchored VWAP回答：从某次重要事件发生那一刻算起，截至目前所有成交的平均成本在哪里？它直接画在主图K线上。新手常为了凑出“刚好踩在线上反弹”的视觉效果而事后随意挪动锚点。

English introduction

Anchored VWAP (AVWAP, a drawing tool that calculates the cumulative volume-weighted average price starting from any specific user-selected candle) asks what the volume-weighted average transaction price has been since a chosen key event (such as an earnings gap, major swing low, or breakout bar). It plots as a curve on the main price chart from the anchor bar forward. Beginners often drag the anchor around in hindsight until the line happens to touch a recent low.

提出者与来源

Paul Levine；Kevin Haggerty（前期方法） — TradingView 将锚定VWAP工具的发展归于 Paul Levine 在1995–1997年的MIDAS工作，并说明其承接 Kevin Haggerty 在1980年代的VWAP方法；Brian Shannon等人后来推广。

TradingView traces the tool to Paul Levine's 1995–1997 MIDAS work, building on Kevin Haggerty's 1980s VWAP use. Brian Shannon and others later popularized it.

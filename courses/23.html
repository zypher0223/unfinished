Bollinger %B 布林百分比

Bollinger %B（布林百分比，用当前收盘价减布林下轨再除以整条带宽算出的位置指标）回答：如果把布林带下轨标作0、中轨标作0.5、上轨标作1.0，当前的收盘价正处在布林带的哪个相对位置？它在副图画出一根单线。新手最容易犯的误解，是以为%B被锁死在0到1之间，或者把%B=1.0当成“100%概率上涨”。

English introduction

Bollinger %B (Percent B, an indicator dividing Close minus the Lower Band by the Upper Band minus the Lower Band) asks where the current close sits relative to the Bollinger Bands on a scale where the Lower Band is 0, the Middle Band is 0.5, and the Upper Band is 1.0. It plots a single line in a lower pane. Beginners often assume %B cannot go above 1 or below 0, or misread 1.0 as a 100% probability.

提出者与来源

John Bollinger — Bollinger本人官网明确说明他创建了%b，用来表示价格相对布林带的位置；首笔交易日期未核实。

Bollinger states that he created %b to show price's position relative to his bands. First live trading use is unverified.

English written lesson

It labels the left lane line 0 and the right lane line 1.0, with 0.5 down the center; if a car drifts onto the right shoulder outside the lane line, its coordinate simply reads 1.1 or 1.2, and drifting off the left side reads below 0.

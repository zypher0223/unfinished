Fibonacci（斐波那契回撤与扩展，挑一段明确的波段起点和终点、按0.236、0.382、0.5、0.618、0.786等比例切分回调深度或向外投射目标的画图工具）回答：当前这波回调已经退回了前一段涨幅的几成，或者顺势突破后走了前一段路程的几倍？它由用户手动在主图K线上点击绘制。新手常误以为0.618是带有大自然神秘魔力的必弹铁底。

English introduction

Fibonacci Retracements and Extensions (drawing tools that divide a chosen price swing into ratios such as 0.382, 0.50, and 0.618 for pullbacks, or 1.0 and 1.618 for outward projections) ask what fraction of a prior swing a pullback has retraced or how far a continuation leg compares to the prior leg. They are drawn manually across swing anchors on the main price chart. Beginners often treat 0.618 as a mystical floor that price must respect.

提出者与来源

Leonardo Fibonacci（数学来源）；交易应用首创者未确认 — 斐波那契数列并非交易工具本身；不能说中世纪数学家本人发明了股市回撤交易法。交易应用的最早提出和使用者未核实。

Leonardo Fibonacci is associated with the mathematical sequence, not the invention of this trading tool. The first trading application has not been verified.

RCI（排名相关指数，基于斯皮尔曼等级相关系数比较“价格高低排名”与“时间先后排名”是否一致的指标）回答：最近N根K线是不是按时间顺序一步比一步高，或者一步比一步低？它在副图-100到+100之间波动，常见参考线在±80。新手最常忽略的是它只看先后名次而不看实际涨跌了多少钱。

English introduction

RCI (Rank Correlation Index, an oscillator using Spearman rank correlation to compare price ranks with time order) asks how consistently prices have risen or fallen in step-by-step sequence over the last N bars. It plots from -100 to +100 in a lower pane, often with ±80 reference lines. Beginners often forget that RCI ranks only sequence order, completely ignoring dollar move size.

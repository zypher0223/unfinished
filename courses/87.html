TTM Squeeze 波动挤压

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

先看价格是不是突然变得很安静，再看安静结束后往哪边加速。常见做法是比较布林带和肯特纳通道的宽窄，再配一组动量柱。

English introduction

The squeeze looks for unusually quiet price movement, then checks how price moves as activity returns. Common versions compare Bollinger Bands with Keltner Channels and show momentum bars.

提出者与来源

John F. Carter — TTM Squeeze 通常归于 John Carter，并由其教学体系推广；社区脚本（如 LazyBear 的 Squeeze Momentum）与原版并非完全相同；名为 Squeeze Pro 的版本既有 Carter 团队后来推出的商业扩展，也有社区仿写。

TTM Squeeze is conventionally attributed to John Carter and promoted through his teaching. Community scripts such as LazyBear's Squeeze Momentum are not identical to the original; versions named Squeeze Pro include both a later commercial extension from Carter's team and community imitations.

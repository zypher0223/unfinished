ALMA（阿诺勒古均线，用钟形正态分布权重对价格求平均的均线）回答：怎样在让均线看起来更平滑、减少毛刺的同时，尽量把重心放在离现在较近的价格上？它直接画在主图K线上。最常见的误解是以为线条画得越光滑就越能预知未来，或者把设置里的偏移（Offset）和宽窄（Sigma）当成提高胜率的神秘参数。

English introduction

ALMA (Arnaud Legoux Moving Average, a moving average that applies Gaussian bell-curve weights to recent bars) asks how to smooth out price noise while keeping the weight center close to recent bars. It plots as a single smooth line on the main price chart. Beginners often confuse visual smoothness with predictive power or treat Offset and Sigma as win-rate dials.

提出者与来源

Arnaud Legoux & Dimitrios Kouzis-Loukas — 两位共同开发者；TradingView 帮助页把姓拼成 Douzis-Loukas，正确拼写为 Kouzis-Loukas。不能只按指标名称漏掉合作者。

Two co-developers; TradingView's help page misspells the surname as Douzis-Loukas (correct: Kouzis-Loukas). The indicator name alone omits a collaborator.

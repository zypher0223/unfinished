Lightning Network & Ordinals 比特币生态：闪电网络与铭文

我的学习与错题新手路线

比特币主链每个区块容量有限，链上转账要付手续费并等待确认，不适合买一杯咖啡这类小额支付。闪电网络的思路是“先开一张账单，最后结清”：通道里能付出去的金额不超过你锁进去的数量，付款还可能经过别人的通道转发，所以路径上要有足够的余额。很多人用的是托管型闪电钱包，由服务商替你管理通道和私钥，方便但要信任对方。铭文则是另一条路：它让比特币交易携带内容，铭文多的时候会和普通转账抢区块空间、推高手续费。铭文和 NFT 有相同的风险：仿冒、流动性差、价格由少数买家决定。

English introduction

Bitcoin blocks have limited space, and on-chain transfers pay fees and wait for confirmation, which does not suit small payments like a coffee. Lightning works like opening a tab and settling at the end: you can pay no more through a channel than you locked in, and payments may be routed through other people’s channels, so the path needs enough balance. Many people use custodial Lightning wallets, where a provider manages the channels and keys; that is convenient but requires trust. Inscriptions take a different path: they make Bitcoin transactions carry content, and when inscriptions are busy they compete with ordinary transfers for block space and push fees up. Inscriptions share the risks of NFTs: copies, thin liquidity and prices set by a few buyers.

提出者与来源

闪电网络：Joseph Poon 与 Thaddeus Dryja（2016 年论文草案）；铭文：Ordinal Theory，手册作者 Casey Rodarmor 等 — 论文《The Bitcoin Lightning Network: Scalable Off-Chain Instant Payments》的草案日期为 2016 年 1 月 14 日；闪电网络的技术规范 BOLT（Basis of Lightning Technology）仍在持续修订。Ordinal Theory 手册说明可以给单个聪写入任意内容，并通过比特币交易转移。

The draft of “The Bitcoin Lightning Network: Scalable Off-Chain Instant Payments” is dated 14 January 2016; Lightning’s specifications, the BOLTs (Basis of Lightning Technology), are still being revised. The Ordinal Theory handbook explains that individual satoshis can be inscribed with arbitrary content and transferred with Bitcoin transactions.

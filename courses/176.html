同样是“买股票”，在不同市场、不同时间能不能成交、多少钱一手、当天能不能卖出，规则都不一样。开户前先弄清交易时间、最小买卖单位、涨跌限制和费用，能少踩很多不必要的坑。

English introduction

“Buying a stock” works differently depending on the market and the hour: whether orders can fill, the size of one lot, and whether you can sell the same day all vary. Learning the hours, minimum unit, price limits and fees before opening an account avoids many needless traps.

提出者与来源

各交易所与监管机构的规则；无单一创始人 — 纽约证券交易所的前身可追溯到 1792 年的梧桐树协议；上海证券交易所与深圳证券交易所于 1990 年成立。A 股卖出单边印花税自 2023 年 8 月 28 日起减半为 0.05%；美股自 2024 年 5 月 28 日起改为 T+1 结算。

The NYSE traces its roots to the Buttonwood Agreement of 1792; the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges opened in 1990. A-share stamp duty on sales was halved to 0.05% from 28 August 2023, and US stocks moved to T+1 settlement on 28 May 2024.

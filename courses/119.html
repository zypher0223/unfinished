谐波形态

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

用几段折线的长度比例检查某种形态是否成立，比如XABCD。重点是量比例，不是看到像蝴蝶就认定能赚钱。

English introduction

Harmonic patterns test ratios between several price swings, often labeled XABCD. Measuring the legs matters more than resemblance to a butterfly.

提出者与来源

H. M. Gartley（早期形态）；Scott M. Carney（现代体系） — Gartley 于1935年描述相关结构；Carney 后来系统化比例与多种谐波形态。不能把整类形态都说成同一个人首次提出。

Gartley described the early structure in 1935. Carney later formalized ratios and multiple harmonic patterns; the whole family has more than one contributor.

Gaps 缺口

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

缺口是两根K线之间的空白：前一根收完，下一根直接从更高（或更低）的地方开始交易。股票隔夜休市才常见缺口；加密市场 24 小时连续交易，K线之间几乎没有缺口。

English introduction

A gap is an empty space between two bars: one bar ends and the next starts trading higher (or lower). Gaps are common in stocks because the market closes overnight; crypto trades around the clock, so its bars rarely gap.

提出者与来源

经典图表分析（Edwards 与 Magee 等）系统整理 — 突破、持续、衰竭缺口的分类见于 Edwards 与 Magee 的《Technical Analysis of Stock Trends》等经典教材；未确认最早提出者。

The breakaway, runaway and exhaustion classification appears in classics such as Edwards and Magee’s “Technical Analysis of Stock Trends”; the earliest originator is not verified.

ATR 平均真实波幅

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

ATR（平均真实波幅，Average True Range，把每根K线的“最高减最低”以及“与前根收盘价的跳空距离”三者取最大值得出真实波幅TR、再做14期RMA平滑的波动率指标）回答：如果把隔夜跳空缺口也算进来，最近每根K线平均下来大概会上下晃动多少块钱？它在副图画出一根大于0的单线。新手最常犯的错误是以为ATR往上升就代表价格在上涨，或者把ATR=2当成“涨幅2%”。

English introduction

ATR (Average True Range, a volatility indicator that takes the greatest of High-minus-Low, |High-minus-prior Close|, and |Low-minus-prior Close| on each bar and smooths it over 14 bars) asks how many dollars or points a typical candle moves when overnight gaps are included. It plots a single positive line in a lower pane. Beginners often assume a rising ATR means rising prices, or misread ATR 2 as 2%.

提出者与来源

J. Welles Wilder Jr. — Wilder在1978年《New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems》中介绍ATR；首笔实盘记录未核实。

Wilder introduced ATR in his 1978 book New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems. First live use is unverified.

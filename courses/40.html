Price Relative（相对表现 / 相对强度比值线，直接用资产A的收盘价除以基准资产B的收盘价画出的比值曲线）回答：在这段时间里，持有资产A的表现是跑赢了基准B（比值上升），还是跑输了基准B（比值下降）？它可画在主图或副图中，注意它和Wilder那个0到100的单品种动量指标RSI完全不是一回事。新手最容易犯的误解，是以为“比值上升（跑赢大盘）”就等于“持有A通常赚到了钱”。

English introduction

Price Relative (Relative Strength Ratio, a line calculated simply by dividing Asset A's price by Benchmark B's price) asks whether Asset A is outperforming Benchmark B (ratio rising) or underperforming Benchmark B (ratio falling) over time. It plots as a ratio curve and is completely different from Wilder's single-asset RSI oscillator. Beginners often assume a rising A/B ratio means Asset A is making money in absolute terms.

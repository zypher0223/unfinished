RWA: Real-World Assets On-Chain 现实资产上链

早期链上金融主要围绕原生加密资产运转（见本站「Stablecoins Basics 稳定币入门」与「DeFi Lending & Liquidation 去中心化借贷与清算」一课），规模有限且受加密牛熊周期影响大。现实世界资产（RWA）通过特殊目的实体（SPV）托管与合规确权，将国债、房产或应收账款映射为链上代币，引入链下现金流并提升分割结算效率。目前主要卡在三处：链下资产履约依赖中心化托管与审计，代码无法阻止链下违约；跨国证券合规与白名单（KYC）限制了自由组合；长尾资产二级市场深度不足且存在预言机延迟（见本站「Bridges & Oracles 跨链桥与预言机」一课）。

English introduction

Early DeFi protocols operated mostly around crypto-native assets (see the "Stablecoins Basics" and "DeFi Lending & Liquidation" lessons on this site), leaving on-chain yields heavily exposed to crypto market cycles. Real-World Assets (RWA) aim to bridge off-chain traditional assets—such as treasury bills, commercial paper, real estate, or commodities—onto the blockchain via Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs), regulated custodians, and smart contracts. This brings off-chain cash flows onto the chain and enables fractional settlement. Currently, the sector faces three main bottlenecks: first, off-chain asset authenticity and repayment rely heavily on centralized custodians and auditors, as smart contracts cannot prevent off-chain defaults; second, cross-border securities regulations and KYC whitelists limit permissionless composability; third, illiquid underlying assets suffer from shallow secondary markets, redemption delays, and oracle pricing lags (see the "Bridges & Oracles" lesson on this site).

提出者与来源

从法币稳定币到链上国债与 ERC-3643 合规代币标准 — RWA 的早期形态是 2014 年以来出现的法币抵押型稳定币与黄金代币。2020 年起，MakerDAO 等去中心化协议开始探索将链下贸易应收款与房地产抵押贷款纳入储备，2021 年起合规权限代币标准 ERC-3643（原 T-REX）逐步成型。2022 至 2024 年美联储加息周期中，传统无风险美债收益率显著高于链上原生借贷利率，推动代币化国债与货币市场基金成为 RWA 规模增长最快的细分方向。

Early forms of RWA emerged with fiat-backed stablecoins and tokenized gold after 2014. Starting in 2020, protocols like MakerDAO experimented with onboarding off-chain trade receivables and real estate loans as collateral, while the permissioned token standard ERC-3643 (formerly T-REX) took shape. During the 2022–2024 Federal Reserve rate-hiking cycle, rising U.S. Treasury yields outpaced crypto-native lending rates, driving tokenized treasuries and money market funds to become the fastest-growing RWA segment.

English written lesson

ATRP/NATR（百分比平均真实波幅 / 归一化ATR，用ATR除以当前收盘价再乘100%算出的相对波动率指标）回答：最近每根K线的平均真实波幅，占当前价格的百分之几？它在副图以百分比形式画出一根单线。它解决了普通ATR因为价格高低不同而无法跨品种比较的问题。新手常把ATRP=2%误当成“下一根K线确保波动2%”或预期收益率。

English introduction

ATRP/NATR (Average True Range Percent / Normalized ATR, an indicator dividing ATR by the current closing price and multiplying by 100%) asks what percentage of current price a typical candle's true range represents. It plots as a percentage line in a lower pane, allowing direct volatility comparisons across assets at very different price levels. Beginners often misread 2% as a assured next-bar move or expected return.

提出者与来源

ATR由Wilder提出；百分比变体较早见于 John Forman（2006）/ ATR by Wilder; normalized variant documented by John Forman (2006) — ATRP/NATR把ATR按价格归一化，常见公式是ATR÷收盘价×100。可查到的较早出处是 John Forman 发表于《Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities》2006年5月号的《Cross-Market Evaluations With Normalized Average True Range》；这不能证明他最早使用，也不能因ATR出自Wilder就认为这个变体也是Wilder首创。

ATRP/NATR normalize ATR by price, commonly ATR/close×100. The earliest well-documented source found is John Forman's 'Cross-Market Evaluations With Normalized Average True Range' (Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities, May 2006); this does not prove he was first, and ATR's attribution to Wilder does not make Wilder the inventor of the variant.

中文文字教学

如果普通ATR是直接称两个人背了多少斤行李，ATRP就是算行李重量占各自体重的百分比：让100斤的人背20斤和让200斤的人背40斤，算出来的相对负担都是20%。

English written lesson

Whereas standard ATR weighs a backpack in raw kilograms, ATRP expresses the pack's weight as a percentage of the hiker's body weight: a 10 kg pack on a 50 kg person and a 20 kg pack on a 100 kg person both represent a 20% relative load.

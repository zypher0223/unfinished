Covered Calls & Protective Puts 备兑看涨与保护性看跌

如果投资者手里已经持有现货股票或加密资产（见本站「Spot, Margin & Leveraged Contracts 现货、杠杆与合约」一课），期权最常见的用途并不是单纯博弈，而是改造现货持仓的损益分布。备兑看涨通过出让上方大涨空间来换取权利金缓冲；保护性看跌则通过主动掏钱买保险来封死下方暴跌深渊。理解每种策略各自放弃了什么，才不会误判风险。

English introduction

When an investor already holds spot shares or crypto assets (see the "Spot, Margin & Leveraged Contracts" lesson on this site), options are most commonly used to reshape the risk-reward profile of that existing holding rather than speculate in isolation. A Covered Call trades away upside above a strike in exchange for upfront premium income, while a Protective Put pays an upfront premium to establish a hard floor under downside losses. Knowing exactly what each strategy gives up prevents costly surprises.

提出者与来源

伴随 1973 年芝加哥期权交易所（Cboe）标准化股票期权上市而成为机构与个人最基础的两大现货组合策略 — 早在场外期权时代就有现货配合期权的对冲做法；1973 年 Cboe 推出标准化看涨期权、1977 年推出看跌期权后，备兑看涨（常用于机构增强收益，Cboe 于 2002 年推出 BXM 标普 500 备兑看涨指数）与保护性看跌（常被称为 Married Put，并在 Black–Scholes 看跌-看涨平价关系中对应“合成看涨期权”）成为最普及的入门组合。

Following Cboe's introduction of standardized call options in 1973 and put options in 1977, Covered Calls (tracked since 2002 by the Cboe S&P 500 BuyWrite Index, BXM) and Protective Puts (often called Married Puts and linked via put-call parity to synthetic calls) became the foundational stock-overlay strategies.

English written lesson

A Covered Call is like a homeowner collecting a non-refundable deposit in exchange for granting a buyer the right to purchase the house at a fixed cap price: you pocket the deposit today, but forfeit any appreciation above the cap. A Protective Put is like buying property value insurance: you pay an out-of-pocket insurance premium so that even if neighborhood prices collapse, your worst-case sale value is floored at the policy strike.

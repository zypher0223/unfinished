StochRSI 随机 RSI

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

StochRSI（随机RSI，把RSI数值放进它自己近期高低区间里计算位置的指标）回答：当前的RSI读数，在最近N根K线的RSI最高值与最低值之间处于什么相对位置？它在副图0到100（或0到1）之间画快线K和慢线D。最常见的误解是把它和普通RSI混为一谈，以为StochRSI到了0就是价格跌无可跌。

English introduction

StochRSI (Stochastic RSI, an indicator measuring where current RSI sits within its own recent high-low range) asks whether RSI is near the top or bottom of its recent N-bar window. It plots fast K and slow D lines from 0 to 100 (or 0 to 1) in a lower pane. Beginners often confuse it with standard RSI and assume a reading of 0 means price itself is deeply oversold.

提出者与来源

Tushar Chande & Stanley Kroll — 两位作者在 1994 年《The New Technical Trader》中介绍 StochRSI。

The authors introduced StochRSI in The New Technical Trader in 1994.

中文文字教学

如果普通RSI是给运动员当天的体能打分，StochRSI就是看今天这个体能分在他自己最近两周的成绩单里排第几名；哪怕他最近天天状态普通（比如都在55到60分之间），考了55分那天排名也会是倒数第一（0分）。

English written lesson

If standard RSI scores an athlete's daily stamina, StochRSI ranks today's score against the athlete's own scores over the past two weeks; even if stamina stays steady between 55 and 60, a 55 still ranks at the very bottom (0) of that narrow window.

威廉指标也看收盘价在最近高低范围中的位置，只是刻度从0到−100。越接近0，收盘越靠近近期顶部；越接近−100越靠近底部。

English introduction

Williams %R locates the close within a recent price range on a scale from 0 to −100. Near 0 is near the top; near −100 is near the bottom.

提出者与来源

Larry Williams — 官方文档明确将Williams %R归于Larry Williams；本次不确定其第一笔实盘日期。

Official documentation credits Larry Williams with Williams %R. His first live trading use is not established here.

江恩理论回答：如果我们预先规定好“每走1根K线时间对应固定多少元价格变动”作为1×1基准速度，当前价格是沿着比基准更快的斜率走，还是掉到了更慢的斜率下方？它在主图K线上用江恩扇形（Gann Fan）等工具绘制。新手常不去设价格/时间比例，就凭肉眼在显示器上看45度角，或追逐神秘的转折日期。

English introduction

Gann Theory (a charting approach using tools like the Gann Fan and Gann Box to bind price change and elapsed time bars into proportional diagonal lines such as 1×1, 2×1, and 1×2) asks whether price is advancing faster or slower than a chosen price-per-bar baseline speed. It is drawn manually on the main price chart. Beginners often eyeball a visual 45-degree angle on their monitor without locking the chart's price-to-bar scale ratio, or chase mystical turning dates.

提出者与来源

William Delbert Gann — 江恩扇、方格等工具与 W. D. Gann 的价格时间研究相关；历史归属不代表其预测主张已得到验证。

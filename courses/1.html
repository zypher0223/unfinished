我的学习与错题新手路线

SMA（简单移动平均线，把最近N根K线收盘价相加再除以N算出的算术平均线）回答：如果抹平每天的短期上下毛刺，最近一段时间的平均收盘成本是在抬高、降低还是走平？它直接画在主图K线上，日线上的20期代表最近20个交易日，1小时图上的20期代表最近20个小时。新手常误以为20期均线能预测未来20天的价格。

English introduction

SMA (Simple Moving Average, an arithmetic mean that sums the last N closing prices and divides by N) asks whether the recent average closing price is rising, falling, or moving sideways once single-bar noise is smoothed out. It plots directly on the main price chart; a 20-period SMA spans 20 trading days on a daily chart or 20 hours on an hourly chart. Beginners often assume a 20-period SMA forecasts prices 20 bars ahead.

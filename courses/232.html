Phishing Signatures, Address Poisoning & Fake Airdrops 钓鱼签名、地址投毒与假空投

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

很多人以为“只要不主动转账，钱就不会丢”。但在链上，一次授权或签名本身就可以把动用代币的权限交出去。ERC-2612 标准里的 Permit（许可签名，一种不用发交易、只在钱包里签一段消息就能授权的方式）在以太坊官方仓库里是正式标准（Final），它本身是正常功能，却也常被骗子用来伪装成“登录”或“验证”。另一类骗局不碰签名：骗子算出一个和你常用地址开头、结尾几位相同的假地址，往你的转账记录里塞一笔零金额或假代币转账，等你下次从记录里复制地址时转错。

English introduction

Many people think “if I never send a transfer, I can’t lose money.” On-chain, an approval or a signature can hand over spending rights by itself. Permit, defined in ERC-2612 (a way to grant an allowance by signing a message instead of sending a transaction), has Final status in the official Ethereum repository. It is a normal feature, yet scammers often disguise it as a “login” or “verification”. Another scam needs no signature: the scammer generates an address whose first and last characters match one you use, plants a zero-value or fake-token transfer in your history, and waits for you to copy the wrong address next time.

提出者与来源

没有单一发明人：这些手法随 ERC-20 授权、ERC-2612 许可签名和公开转账记录的特点出现 — ERC-20（2015 年提出）定义了 approve 授权；ERC-2612（2020 年提出，作者 Martin Lundfall）让授权可以用离线签名完成，两者在以太坊官方仓库都是 Final 状态。钓鱼和地址投毒是滥用这些正常功能的诈骗手法，不是哪个人提出的技术。

ERC-20 (proposed 2015) defines approve; ERC-2612 (proposed 2020 by Martin Lundfall) lets an approval be made with an off-chain signature. Both are Final in the official Ethereum repository. Phishing and address poisoning are scams that abuse these normal features, not techniques anyone proposed.

English written lesson

A phishing signature is like being handed a “sign-in sheet” that is really a power of attorney; address poisoning is like someone adding a contact to your phone with your friend’s name and a number that differs only in the middle digits; a fake airdrop is a “you’ve won” letter in your mailbox pointing to a website.

Averaging Down vs Adding to Winners 补仓与加仓

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

补仓让“回本线”变近，所以感觉很舒服；但它是在已经被市场否定的判断上加大赌注。浮盈加仓则是在被市场证明对的方向上加码，难的是需要勇气在更高的价格再买。

English introduction

Averaging down brings your break-even closer, so it feels comforting, but it raises your bet on an idea the market has already rejected. Adding to winners raises your bet in the direction the market has confirmed; the hard part is having the nerve to buy again at a higher price.

提出者与来源

未确认唯一创始人；与赌场的“马丁格尔”加倍法思路相近 — 亏损后加倍下注以求一次回本的“马丁格尔”策略流行于 18 世纪的法国；金字塔加仓常被归于 Jesse Livermore 等老一代投机者的做法，但最早使用者未确认。习思cis在书中把补仓称为“最差的技巧”。

The martingale, doubling the stake after each loss to recover everything with one win, was popular in 18th-century France. Pyramiding is often attributed to old-school speculators such as Jesse Livermore, though the earliest user is unverified. cis calls averaging down “the worst technique” in his book.

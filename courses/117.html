艾略特波浪理论回答：如果当前这轮趋势符合经典的五浪推进与三浪调整模型，它目前处于哪个阶段，且哪一个具体价位被跌破会当场证伪这个数浪假设？它在主图波段上手动标记。新手最容易犯的错，是只记住“五上三下”口诀，数错了就事后擦掉重标、永远不认错。

English introduction

Elliott Wave Theory (a structural framework classifying trend-following moves into 5-wave impulses labeled 1-2-3-4-5 and counter-trend moves into 3-wave corrections labeled A-B-C) asks how a multi-swing move fits into an impulse-and-correction cycle and which exact price level falsifies that wave count. It is labeled manually across swing turns on the main chart. Beginners often memorize '5 up, 3 down' as a prophecy and endlessly relabel broken counts in hindsight.

提出者与来源

Ralph Nelson Elliott — Elliott 在1930年代发展波浪原理，1938年出版《The Wave Principle》；Frost、Prechter 后来广泛推广。

Elliott developed the Wave Principle in the 1930s and published The Wave Principle in 1938; Frost and Prechter later popularized it.

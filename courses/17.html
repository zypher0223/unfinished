Momentum 动量指标

我的学习与错题新手路线

Momentum（动量指标，简称MTM，直接用当前收盘价减去N根K线前收盘价算出的差值指标）回答：跟正好N根K线之前相比，当前价格涨了或跌了多少块钱（绝对价差）？它在副图围绕0线（部分比值版本围绕100线）波动。新手最常把它和上一课算百分比的ROC混为一谈，或者跨不同价位的品种直接比动量数字大小。

English introduction

Momentum (MTM, an indicator commonly subtracting the close N bars ago from the current close) asks how many raw dollars or points price has gained or lost compared with N bars back. It oscillates around 0 (or around 100 in ratio versions) in a lower pane. Beginners often confuse raw price-difference MTM with percentage-based ROC or compare raw point values across assets with different prices.

Price-difference momentum is a general comparison. Momentum-investing researchers should not automatically be labeled inventors of MTM.

中文文字教学

如果ROC是算“比10秒前多跑了百分之几”，MTM就是直接拿尺子量“比10秒前多跑了多少米”；但大象迈一步前進3米和蚂蚁爬一步前進3厘米，不能因为3米大于3厘米就说大象比平时更拼命。

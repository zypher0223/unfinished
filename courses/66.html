Relative Vigor Index（相对活力指数，简称RVI，用收盘减开盘的实体位移除以最高减最低的全天波幅并做平滑的指标）回答：在最近每根K线的上下折腾范围里，收盘价到底能比开盘价扎实推进多少成？它在副图围绕0线画出RVI主线和慢速信号线。新手最容易把它和同样缩写为RVI的“相对波动指数”搞混。

English introduction

The Relative Vigor Index (RVI, an oscillator comparing close-minus-open body movement to high-minus-low total range across smoothed bars) asks how much net open-to-close progress price achieves relative to each bar's full swing. It plots a main line and signal line around zero in a lower pane. Beginners frequently confuse it with the Relative Volatility Index, which shares the 'RVI' acronym.

提出者与来源

John F. Ehlers — John F. Ehlers 在《Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities》2002年1月号的文章《Relative Vigor Index》中提出这个经滤波平滑的 RVI；他也说明“收盘减开盘、除以最高减最低”的思路来自 Jim Waters 与 Larry Williams 1972年的累积/派发振荡指标。

John F. Ehlers introduced the filter-smoothed RVI in 'Relative Vigor Index' (Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities, January 2002); he notes the close-minus-open over high-minus-low idea revives Jim Waters and Larry Williams' 1972 accumulation/distribution oscillator.

DeFi Lending & Liquidation 去中心化借贷与清算

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

链上借贷不看信用记录，只看抵押品；规则写在合约里，自动执行，不会打电话提醒你补仓。抵押品价格下跌、借来的资产价格上涨，都会让健康因子下降，一旦跌破 1 就会被清算，还要额外付出清算罚金。

English introduction

On-chain lending ignores credit history and looks only at collateral; the rules are in code, execute automatically, and nobody calls you to top up. A falling collateral price or a rising borrowed-asset price both cut the health factor, and once it drops below 1 you are liquidated and pay a penalty on top.

提出者与来源

MakerDAO（2017 年 DAI）开创超额抵押借贷；Compound（2018）与 Aave（2020 年前身 ETHLend 转型）推广资金池借贷 — MakerDAO 于 2017 年 12 月推出以 ETH 超额抵押生成的稳定币 DAI。Compound 于 2018 年上线资金池借贷，2020 年 6 月发放治理代币 COMP，引发“流动性挖矿”热潮（DeFi Summer）。2020 年 3 月 12 日的暴跌中，以太坊拥堵导致 MakerDAO 部分清算拍卖以接近零的价格成交，成为清算机制设计的重要教训。

MakerDAO launched DAI, a stablecoin minted against overcollateralized ETH, in December 2017. Compound launched pooled lending in 2018 and its COMP token in June 2020, sparking the “liquidity mining” boom (DeFi Summer). In the 12 March 2020 crash, Ethereum congestion let some MakerDAO liquidation auctions clear near zero, a key lesson for liquidation design.

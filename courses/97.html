Williams Fractals回答：按“五根K线中间最高或最低”的固定标准，近期走出了哪些微型局部顶底？它直接画在主图K线顶底。新手最容易犯的误解，是看到箭头画在某根K线头顶，就以为在那根K线当天就已经发出了信号。

English introduction

Williams Fractals (an indicator marking a 5-bar local peak when the middle 3rd bar's high exceeds the 2 highs to its left and 2 highs to its right, and vice versa for lows) ask where minor 5-bar swing highs and lows have formed. They plot small arrows above highs and below lows on the main price chart. Beginners often assume an arrow sitting above Bar 3 appeared while Bar 3 was forming, overlooking the 2-bar confirmation delay.

提出者与来源

Bill Williams — 威廉分形是 Bill Williams 交易体系中的局部高低点识别工具；不要与数学分形理论的创始归属混为一谈。

Williams Fractals belong to Bill Williams's trading framework; this does not attribute mathematical fractal theory to him.

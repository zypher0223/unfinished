MACD-V

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

MACD-V（波动率归一化MACD，由Spiroglou提出、把传统MACD差值除以同期ATR真实波幅再乘100的指标）回答：当前快慢均线的差距，相当于平时每天正常波动幅度的百分之多少？它在副图围绕0线画出主线和信号线。新手最常犯的误解是以为名字里的“V”代表成交量（Volume），其实它代表波动率（Volatility）。

English introduction

MACD-V (Volatility-Normalized MACD, proposed by Alex Spiroglou, dividing standard MACD by ATR and multiplying by 100) asks how large the gap between fast and slow moving averages is when measured in units of typical bar volatility. It plots a main line and signal line around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often mistake the 'V' for Volume rather than Volatility.

提出者与来源

Alexandros (Alex) Spiroglou — 原作者 MACD-V 论文署名 Alex Spiroglou；基于 Appel 的 MACD 改造。

The original MACD-V paper is by Alex Spiroglou, adapting Appel's MACD.

中文文字教学

如果传统MACD只报告“快车比慢车领先了10米”，MACD-V则会换算成“领先了相当于平时几步远”：对步幅1米的人来说10米是10步，对步幅5米的巨人来说10米只有2步；但领先几步依然是过去均值的总结，不能预言下一秒谁跑得快。

English written lesson

Whereas standard MACD reports a lead in raw meters, MACD-V reports how many typical strides that lead equals: a 10-meter gap is 10 strides when steps are 1 meter, but only 2 strides when steps are 5 meters; however, counting strides still summarizes past bars rather than predicting the next step.

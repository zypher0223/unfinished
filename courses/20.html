Bull/Bear Power 多空力量

Bull/Bear Power（多空力量指标，艾尔德射线Elder-Ray体系中用当根最高价减13期EMA算多头力量、用最低价减13期EMA算空头力量的指标）回答：在当前这根K线里，买方最高能把价格推到平均成本线以上多远，卖方最低能把价格砸到平均成本线以下多远？它在副图围绕0线显示。新手最容易犯的错，是没看清软件画的是拆开的两条柱子还是合并相加后的单条BBP线。

English introduction

Bull/Bear Power (from Alexander Elder's Elder-Ray system, measuring High minus 13-bar EMA for Bull Power and Low minus 13-bar EMA for Bear Power) asks how far the bar's high stretched above the EMA baseline and how far the bar's low stretched below it. It plots around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often fail to check whether their platform plots Bull and Bear separately or sums them into one combined BBP line.

提出者与来源

Alexander Elder（Elder-Ray体系） / Alexander Elder (Elder-Ray) — 多空力量与Elder的Elder-Ray体系相关；TradingView的BBP将Bull Power与Bear Power相加，不能把所有同名版本视为同一个公式。最早实盘使用未核实。

Bull/Bear Power is associated with Elder's Elder-Ray system. TradingView BBP sums the two components; similarly named versions may differ. First live use is unverified.

English written lesson

If the 13-bar EMA is the center line of a tug-of-war field, Bull Power measures the farthest reach to the right and Bear Power measures the farthest reach to the left during that round; however, a brief reach does not tell you where the rope finished at the whistle.

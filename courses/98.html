English introduction

ZigZag (an overlay filter that connects swing highs and lows with straight lines only after price reverses by at least a chosen percentage or point threshold) asks what the major historical price swings look like once smaller fluctuations below the threshold are ignored. It plots directly on the main price chart. The most dangerous beginner mistake is treating the latest, actively repainting leg as a live trading signal.

Divergence 背离

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

背离就是价格和指标“说的不一样”：价格创新高，指标却没跟上。它提醒上涨的力度在减弱，但不是卖出指令；背离可以连续出现好几次，价格仍然继续涨。

English introduction

Divergence is price and the indicator “disagreeing”: price makes a new high but the indicator does not. It warns that momentum is fading, but it is not a sell instruction; divergence can repeat several times while price keeps rising.

提出者与来源

未确认唯一创始人；随 RSI、MACD 等振荡指标流行 — 价格与动量指标的背离在 Wilder（1978 年发表 RSI）等作者的著作中已有讨论；“隐藏背离”的说法后来才流行，最早提出者未确认。

Divergence between price and momentum was discussed by authors such as Wilder (who published RSI in 1978); the term “hidden divergence” spread later and its originator is unverified.

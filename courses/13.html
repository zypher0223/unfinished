Stochastic/KD（随机指标，通过计算当前收盘价在最近N根K线最高价与最低价区间内的相对位置百分数来观察短期强弱的指标）回答：如果把最近14根K线的最低点算作0分、最高点算作100分，今天的收盘价停在几分的位置？它在副图0到100之间画出较快的K线和较慢的D线。新手常把读数20误当成“价格很便宜”，把80当成“价格太贵必跌”。

English introduction

The Stochastic Oscillator (KD, an indicator measuring where the current close sits as a percentage of the recent N-bar high-to-low range) asks whether price closed near the top or bottom of its recent trading window. It plots the faster %K line and slower %D average between 0 and 100 in a lower pane. Beginners often mistake a reading below 20 for 'cheap value' or above 80 for a assured drop.

提出者与来源

George C. Lane — TradingView将随机指标开发归于1950年代的George Lane；未核实第一笔交易使用。不同地区KD/KDJ平滑细节可能不同。

TradingView credits George Lane with developing Stochastic in the 1950s. First live use is unverified, and regional KD/KDJ variants can differ.

English written lesson

If the recent 14-bar low is the 1st floor and the 14-bar high is the 10th floor, Stochastic simply reports which floor today's close finished on; during a steady climb up a mountain, finishing near the top floor every day is normal.

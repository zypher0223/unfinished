Volatility Stop回答：如果市场平时安静时把止损参考留窄一点、剧烈颠簸时把止损参考留宽一点，当前的跟踪边线在哪？它直接画在主图K线上下方。新手常误以为它可以告诉你“这只标的到底值多少钱”，其实它只管风控距离不管估值。

English introduction

Volatility Stop (a trailing exit reference that widens its buffer when recent price swings are large and narrows it when swings are small) asks how much breathing room a position needs based on recent market noise. It plots on the main price chart. Beginners often forget that widening a stop distance during high volatility requires reducing position size to keep dollar risk constant.

Volatility stops are a family of methods using ATR, standard deviation or other estimates. A sole first user has not been established.

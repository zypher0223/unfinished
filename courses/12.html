MACD（指数平滑异同移动平均线，通过12期快EMA减26期慢EMA得到MACD线、再配9期信号线与差值柱状图的指标）回答：短期均线与中期均线之间的距离是在拉开还是在收拢？它在副图围绕0线画出快慢双线和红绿柱子。新手最常犯的误解，是看到0线上方的正柱子缩短了一点，就以为价格已经在下跌了。

English introduction

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence, an indicator subtracting a 26-bar EMA from a 12-bar EMA, paired with a 9-bar EMA signal line and a difference histogram) asks whether the gap between short- and medium-term moving averages is widening or narrowing. It plots two lines and a histogram around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often assume shrinking positive histogram bars mean price is falling.

提出者与来源

Gerald Appel；柱状图由Thomas Aspray补充 / Gerald Appel; histogram added by Thomas Aspray — Appel在1970年代开发MACD；TradingView记载Aspray于1986年加入柱状图。首笔交易记录未核实。

Appel developed MACD in the 1970s. TradingView credits Aspray with adding its histogram in 1986. First trade records were not verified.

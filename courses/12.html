MACD

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

MACD看快均线和慢均线相差多少，再用一条较慢的信号线跟踪这个差。柱子显示两条线之间的差距，帮助看涨跌力量有没有加快。

English introduction

MACD measures the gap between a fast and a slow average. A signal line smooths that gap, and the histogram shows the gap between MACD and its signal.

提出者与来源

Gerald Appel；柱状图由Thomas Aspray补充 / Gerald Appel; histogram added by Thomas Aspray — Appel在1970年代开发MACD；TradingView记载Aspray于1986年加入柱状图。首笔交易记录未核实。

Appel developed MACD in the 1970s. TradingView credits Aspray with adding its histogram in 1986. First trade records were not verified.

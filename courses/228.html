GameFi: On-Chain Game Economies 链上游戏经济

我的学习与错题新手路线

传统游戏中，玩家购买的皮肤与装备保存在游戏公司中心化数据库里，无法跨平台自由转让，停服即归零。GameFi 用非同质化代币（ERC-721 或 ERC-1155 正式标准 NFT，见本站「Smart Contracts & Token Approvals 智能合约与代币授权」一课）记录角色与道具所有权，并用同质化代币构建开放经济。早期“边玩边赚（Play-to-Earn, P2E）”多采用双代币模型：新玩家买角色 NFT 入场，每日对战产出无上限的游戏效用币，而消耗场景主要是老玩家用效用币“繁殖”新角色 NFT 卖给后来者。这种依赖外部增量资金的模型极易触发“死亡螺旋”：一旦新玩家增长放缓，新角色需求下降，老玩家便停止消耗并集中抛售效用币；币价暴跌拉长回本周期，进一步劝退新人并引发存量玩家抛售 NFT（见本站「Tokenomics: Supply, FDV & Unlocks 代币经济学：供应、FDV 与解锁」一课）。后续行业向两个方向演进：一是强调娱乐消耗的“可玩优先（Play-and-Own）”；二是将核心规则部署在合约上的“全链游戏（Fully On-Chain Games）”，但仍面临链上延迟与经济平衡难题。

English introduction

In traditional video games, skins and items bought or earned by players sit inside the publisher's centralized database and vanish if the servers shut down. GameFi uses non-fungible tokens (such as official Ethereum standards ERC-721 and ERC-1155; see the "Smart Contracts & Token Approvals" lesson on this site) for item ownership and fungible tokens for in-game currencies. Early Play-to-Earn (P2E) games popularized a dual-token model: new players bought character NFTs to enter, earned uncapped utility tokens daily through battles, and spent those tokens primarily to 'breed' new character NFTs to sell to newer entrants. Because this loop relied on continuous new capital inflows, it proved vulnerable to a reflexive 'death spiral': as soon as new player growth slowed, demand for newly bred NFTs fell, causing existing players to dump utility tokens on the market instead of breeding; falling token prices lengthened payback periods, deterring new entrants and triggering a simultaneous collapse in both token and NFT floor prices (see the "Tokenomics: Supply, FDV & Unlocks" lesson on this site). Subsequent iterations have explored 'Play-and-Own' designs focused on entertainment sinks and 'Fully On-Chain Games' where game logic runs directly in smart contracts, though balancing latency and sustainable economics remains an ongoing challenge.

提出者与来源

从 2017 年 CryptoKitties 与 2020 年 GameFi 概念提出到 2021 年 P2E 兴衰 — 2017 年底上线的《加密猫》（CryptoKitties）首次将 ERC-721 非同质化代币用于链上养成繁殖游戏。2020 年 9 月，Yearn 创始人 Andre Cronje 在社交媒体上使用“GameFi”一词，使它在加密圈流行开来（此前已有业内人士用过类似说法）；2021 年，由 Sky Mavis 开发的《Axie Infinity》将双代币“边玩边赚（P2E）”模式推向高峰，但也因其奖励代币 SLP 过度通胀与 2022 年初新用户放缓而经历了典型的死亡螺旋，促使行业反思纯打金模型的局限性。

Launched in late 2017, CryptoKitties pioneered ERC-721 NFTs for breeding mechanics on Ethereum. In September 2020, Andre Cronje's use of the term 'GameFi' on social media helped popularise it (similar terms had appeared earlier), and in 2021 Sky Mavis's Axie Infinity popularized the dual-token Play-to-Earn (P2E) model before experiencing a textbook token inflation and retention death spiral in early 2022.

English written lesson

An early P2E economy is like a farm where visitors buy hens to lay eggs, and the main use for eggs is hatching more hens to sell to new visitors: as long as a line of new buyers forms outside, everyone appears profitable—but the moment new visitor growth stalls, everyone dumps eggs at the market simultaneously, crashing the price of both eggs and hens.

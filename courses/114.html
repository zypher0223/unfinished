English introduction

Classic Chart Patterns (a structural method that groups multi-swing candle outlines into formations such as double tops/bottoms, head-and-shoulders, triangles, wedges, and flags) ask how buyers and sellers are consolidating across multiple swings and which boundary break confirms a resolution. They are mapped across swing highs and lows on the main price chart. Beginners often jump in halfway through a developing shape before the neckline or boundary ever breaks.

提出者与来源

多位历史作者发展；单一创始人未确认 / Developed by multiple chartists — 头肩、双顶、三角形等不是同一个人的单项发明；Robert D. Edwards 与 John Magee 的经典教材是重要整理来源，不能将整理者当作全部形态的首次使用者。

Head-and-shoulders, double tops and triangles are not one person’s single invention. Robert D. Edwards and John Magee’s classic textbook is an important systematic reference, not proof that they first used every pattern.

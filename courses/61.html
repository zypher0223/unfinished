TRIX（三重指数平滑移动平均指标，把价格连续做三次指数平滑后再计算单根变动率的动量指标）回答：剔除短线来回抖动后，较长周期的价格平滑重心每根K线是在加速上移还是下移？它在副图围绕0线画出TRIX线（部分版本配信号线）。新手最常误解的是看它线条极其平滑，就以为跟着它转向毫无风险。

English introduction

TRIX (Triple Exponential Average, a momentum indicator showing the percentage rate of change of a triple-smoothed moving average) asks whether the broad, noise-filtered price baseline is rising or falling from bar to bar. It plots around a zero line in a lower pane. Beginners often mistake its ultra-smooth curve for low trading risk, overlooking the lag caused by triple smoothing.

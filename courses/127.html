供需区分析回答：过去哪一小片价格整理基地曾爆发过多空剧烈失衡的快速离开，且价格第一次回到这片基地时是否还会减速或反弹？向上快速离开的起点标为需求区候选，向下快速离开的标为供给区候选。。

English introduction

Supply and Demand Zone Analysis (a charting method that marks compact consolidation bases from which price previously departed with a rapid, wide-range impulse move) asks where a sharp historical imbalance launched and how price behaves on its first return to that base. A fast upward departure leaves a candidate demand zone; a fast downward departure leaves a candidate supply zone. Beginners often assume a drawn rectangle proves resting institutional limit orders are still waiting inside.

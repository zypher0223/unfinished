Cutting Losses 止损与认错

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

止损不是“认输”，而是给每一笔交易设好亏损的上限。新手最常见的亏法不是小亏太多，而是一笔不肯止损的单子越拖越大。先决定错在哪里，再决定买不买。

English introduction

A stop is not “giving up”; it caps how much any one trade can cost. Beginners rarely lose from too many small losses; they lose when one trade they refuse to exit keeps growing. Decide where you would be wrong before deciding to buy.

提出者与来源

未确认唯一创始人；交易界长期流传的风险控制原则 — “截断亏损、让利润奔跑”是交易界长期流传的说法，最早出处未确认。固定比例止损的常见版本如 William O’Neil 在《How to Make Money in Stocks》中提出的 7%–8% 规则；本课参考的习思cis则说自己没有固定数值止损线，判断一小时后可能会跌就卖。

“Cut your losses and let your profits run” is a long-standing trading maxim whose first source is unverified. A common fixed-percentage version is William O’Neil’s 7–8% rule in “How to Make Money in Stocks”; cis, whose book this lesson draws on, says he keeps no fixed numeric stop and sells when he expects a fall within the hour.

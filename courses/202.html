Bridges & Oracles 跨链桥与预言机

加密史上损失最大的几次黑客事件都发生在跨链桥：它们锁着巨额资产，又依赖少数签名者或复杂代码。预言机则是智能合约的“眼睛”，眼睛被蒙住，合约就会照着错误的价格执行。理解这两类基础设施，就能看懂很多“协议被盗”新闻背后的原因。

English introduction

Several of the largest hacks in crypto history hit bridges: they hold huge sums and depend on a few signers or complex code. Oracles are a smart contract’s eyes; blindfold them and the contract executes at the wrong price. Understanding both explains many “protocol hacked” headlines.

提出者与来源

标志性事件：Wormhole（2022 年 2 月）、Ronin（2022 年 3 月）跨链桥被盗 — 2022 年 2 月，Wormhole 因签名验证漏洞被凭空铸造约 12 万枚包装 ETH，损失约 3.2 亿美元；2022 年 3 月，Axie Infinity 使用的 Ronin 桥 9 个验证者中有 5 个私钥被攻破，约 17.36 万 ETH 和 2550 万 USDC（约 6.25 亿美元）被盗，美国财政部随后将其归因于朝鲜 Lazarus 组织。预言机网络 Chainlink 的白皮书发布于 2017 年。

In February 2022 a signature-verification flaw let an attacker mint about 120,000 wrapped ETH on Wormhole, a loss of about USD 320 million. In March 2022 five of the nine validator keys of Axie Infinity’s Ronin bridge were compromised and about 173,600 ETH plus 25.5 million USDC (about USD 625 million) were stolen; the US Treasury later attributed it to North Korea’s Lazarus group. The Chainlink oracle network’s white paper appeared in 2017.

中文文字教学

跨链桥像在两地之间寄存兑换：你把金条存进 A 城保险库，在 B 城拿到一张提货券；保险库被盗，提货券就成了废纸。预言机像商场的价格公告牌，有人偷偷改了牌价，收银台就按错的价格收钱。

English written lesson

A bridge is like depositing gold in a vault in city A and receiving a claim ticket in city B; if the vault is robbed, the ticket is worthless. An oracle is like a mall’s price board: if someone quietly changes the board, the till charges the wrong price.

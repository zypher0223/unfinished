PMO（价格动量振荡器，DecisionPoint提出的对单根涨跌幅百分比做双重自定义指数平滑的指标）回答：把每根K线的涨跌幅百分比连续平滑两遍后，近期涨跌速度是在增强还是放缓？它在副图围绕0线画出PMO主线和10周期信号线。新手最容易犯的误解，是看到PMO在0线下方往上拐，就以为价格已经在上涨了。

English introduction

PMO (Price Momentum Oscillator, DecisionPoint's indicator that double-smooths bar-to-bar percentage price changes) asks whether smoothed percentage momentum is strengthening or weakening. It plots a PMO line and a 10-bar EMA signal line around zero in a lower pane. Beginners often assume a rising PMO below zero means price is rising, when it may only mean price is falling more slowly.

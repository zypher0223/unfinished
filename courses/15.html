CCI 顺势指标

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

CCI（顺势指标，Commodity Channel Index，用当根典型价格减去近期平均典型价、再除以0.015倍平均绝对偏差的指标）回答：跟最近20根K线的平均水平以及平时的正常波动幅度相比，今天的价格偏离得有多远？它在副图围绕0线波动，常见参考线在+100和-100。新手常误以为CCI像RSI一样被封死在-100到+100之间，其实它没有上下限。

English introduction

CCI (Commodity Channel Index, an unbounded oscillator dividing the distance between typical price and its moving average by 0.015 times the mean absolute deviation) asks how far current price has strayed from its recent average relative to normal bar dispersion. It plots around zero in a lower pane with reference lines at +100 and -100. Beginners often assume CCI is capped at ±100 like RSI, whereas CCI has no upper or lower bound.

提出者与来源

Donald Lambert — Lambert于1980年在Commodities Magazine介绍CCI；未核实首笔实盘使用。

Lambert introduced CCI in Commodities Magazine in 1980. First live trading use is unverified.

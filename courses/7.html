DEMA 双指数均线

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

DEMA不是简单画两条EMA，而是把EMA和它再次平滑后的结果相减，做成一条跟价格更紧的线。

English introduction

DEMA is one calculated line, not simply two EMAs. It subtracts a second smoothing from twice the first EMA to reduce delay.

提出者与来源

Patrick G. Mulloy — 1994年《Stocks & Commodities》文章《Smoothing Data with Faster Moving Averages》介绍DEMA；未核实最早实盘使用日期。

Mulloy's 1994 Stocks & Commodities article introduced DEMA. Its first live trading use was not verified.

我的学习与错题新手路线

以太坊上的一笔交易会记下发送方（from）、接收方（to）、金额（value）、序号（nonce）和手续费（gas，执行交易要付的费用）等字段，并广播给全网，所以人人可查。区块浏览器是“查账工具”，不是钱包，它不保管你的私钥，也不能替你撤回转账。浏览器和数据公司会给一些地址贴“标签”，比如“某交易所热钱包”“巨鲸”（持有大量资产的地址），这些标签是他们根据线索做的推断，可能出错。看资金流向时还要注意：转入交易所不等于要卖出，交易所自己在冷钱包（离线保存）和热钱包（在线、用于日常提现）之间调拨，也会产生大额转账。

English introduction

An Ethereum transaction records fields such as the sender (from), recipient (to), amount (value), sequence number (nonce) and gas (the fee paid to execute it), and is broadcast to the whole network, so anyone can look it up. A block explorer is a lookup tool, not a wallet: it does not hold your private keys and cannot reverse a transfer. Explorers and data firms attach labels to some addresses, such as “exchange hot wallet” or “whale” (an address holding a large amount); these are their inferences and can be wrong. When reading flows, remember that a deposit to an exchange is not the same as a sale: exchanges also move funds between cold wallets (kept offline) and hot wallets (online, used for withdrawals), which creates large transfers too.

没有单一发明人：区块浏览器和链上分析随公链的公开账本出现 — 公链的交易要广播给全网验证，记录天然公开；区块浏览器把这些记录整理成网页，数据平台再把它们整理成可以用 SQL 查询的表。地址标签由各家公司自行维护，口径不统一。

Public-chain transactions are broadcast to the whole network for validation, so the record is public by design; block explorers present it as web pages, and data platforms turn it into tables you can query with SQL. Address labels are maintained by each company and follow no common standard.

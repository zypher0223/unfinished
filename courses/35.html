PVT回答：如果不再像OBV那样不论涨多涨少都一刀切加减全天成交量，而是按涨跌幅大小按比例计入成交量，累计出来的量价重心朝哪边走？它在副图画一条累计线。新手常误以为PVT上升就证明所谓的“聪明钱”在暗中吸筹。

English introduction

PVT (Price Volume Trend, a cumulative line that multiplies each bar's percentage close-to-close change by that bar's volume and adds it to a running total) asks how cumulative volume trends when each bar's volume is weighted by the percentage size of the price move rather than added all-or-nothing like OBV. It plots as a single line in a lower pane. Beginners often assume a rising PVT proves secret institutional buying.

中文文字教学

如果OBV是只要赢1分就把全场观众全算作奖金，PVT则是按“赢了百分之几”去乘全场观众人数来发奖金：涨10%那天记入的成交量权重，是同样成交量下微涨1%那天的10倍。

English written lesson

Whereas OBV awards 100% of the crowd's points for winning by a single point, PVT scales the points by the percentage margin of victory: a 10% gain on 1,000 volume adds ten times as much to PVT as a 1% gain on 1,000 volume.

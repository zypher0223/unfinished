Ulcer Index（溃疡指数，简称UI，专门统计最近N根K线里每根收盘价相对近期最高收盘价回撤百分比的均方根指标）回答：在最近这段窗口里，如果一直持有该资产，经历的下跌深度和被套时间合起来有多熬人？它在副图0以上画一条单线。新手常把它和标准差混为一谈，或者以为UI回落就代表价格已经涨回来了。

English introduction

The Ulcer Index (UI, a downside volatility gauge calculating the root-mean-square of percentage drawdowns below recent rolling highs) asks how deep and how prolonged recent pullbacks from peak prices have been. It plots a single line from 0 upward in a lower pane. Beginners often confuse it with standard deviation or assume a falling UI always means price has rallied back to its old high.

提出者与来源

Peter Martin；Byron McCann（共同出版） — Peter Martin 于1987年设计，1989年与 Byron McCann 共同出版介绍。

Devised by Peter Martin in 1987 and published by Martin and Byron McCann in 1989.

English written lesson

Unlike standard deviation, which counts both uphill climbs and downhill drops as volatility, the Ulcer Index measures only the depth and duration of potholes below the recent peak; however, measuring the pothole you just crossed does not warn you of the next one.

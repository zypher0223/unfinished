这三类骗局的共同点是：许诺稳定、高额、“不会亏”的收益，并让你看到账面上的数字在涨。美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）提醒留意三个信号：第一，有人自称商家、政府机构或“投资老师”，催你先转一笔加密货币过去，才能买东西、“保护资金”或开始投资——正规商家和机构不会强迫你预先用加密货币付款（你自己选择用加密货币买东西是另一回事）；第二，对方向你承诺赚钱或高回报；第三，网上认识的恋爱对象开始给你投资建议。杀猪盘里的“平台”往往是骗子自己控制的网站，账面收益只是数据库里的数字；你申请提现时，对方会要求先交“税费”“保证金”或“解冻费”。跑路项目则常见于新发的代币：合约里留着增发或禁止卖出的权限，或者项目方掌握大部分流动性，随时可以撤走。

English introduction

All three promise steady, high or risk-free returns and let you watch a balance grow. The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) points to three warning signs. First, someone claiming to be a business, a government agency or an “investment coach” pushes you to send cryptocurrency in advance in order to buy something, “protect your money” or start investing; legitimate businesses and agencies do not demand advance payment in cryptocurrency (choosing to pay with crypto yourself is a different matter). Second, they promise profits or big returns. Third, someone you met through online dating starts giving you investment advice. In pig butchering the “platform” is often a site the scammer controls, and the balance is just a number in their database; when you ask to withdraw, you are told to pay “tax”, a “deposit” or an “unlock fee” first. Rug pulls are common with new tokens: the contract keeps powers to mint or block selling, or the team controls most of the liquidity and can pull it at any time.

提出者与来源

庞氏骗局得名于 1920 年代美国的 Charles Ponzi；“杀猪盘”是中文网络对恋爱加投资骗局的俗称 — 用新投资者的钱支付旧投资者的手法早于 Ponzi，但因他在 1920 年的大规模骗局而得名。“杀猪盘”一词把受害人比作被“养肥再宰”的猪，后被英文媒体和执法机构直译为 pig butchering。跑路（rug pull）是加密圈对项目方卷款撤离的俗称，没有单一提出者。

Paying old investors with new investors’ money predates Ponzi, but the name comes from his large 1920 scheme. “Pig butchering” likens victims to pigs fattened before slaughter; English-language media and law enforcement adopted the literal translation. “Rug pull” is crypto slang for a team running off with funds and has no single originator.

English written lesson

Pig butchering is a “friend” of several months who takes you to a “bank” only they know, where your passbook grows daily but the counter is always “under maintenance”; a rug pull is a mall with a grand-opening sale that empties overnight after selling prepaid cards; a Ponzi scheme is pass-the-parcel, and whoever holds it when no new player joins loses.

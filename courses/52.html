LSMA（最小二乘均线，用线性回归直线拟合近期价格并连起右端点的曲线）回答：如果给最近N根K线画一条总体最贴近它们的直线，这条直线在当前时刻指向哪个价位？它直接画在主图K线上。新手常误以为“数学最佳拟合”就等于“未来最准预测”，忽略了它拟合的只是已经走完的过去。

English introduction

LSMA (Least Squares Moving Average, a curve connecting the right endpoints of rolling linear regression lines) asks where a best-fit straight line through the last N bars points on the current bar. It plots directly on the main price chart. Beginners often confuse a mathematically optimal fit of past prices with an accurate forecast of future prices.

VWAP回答：从今天开盘算起到现在这一刻，场内所有成交按量加权算出来的平均成交价是多少？它直接画在日内主图K线上，且每个新交易日开盘时重新归零起算。新手常把它当成全市场所有持仓者的总成本。

English introduction

VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price, an intraday benchmark that accumulates Typical Price times Volume from the start of the session and divides by cumulative session volume) asks what the volume-weighted average transaction price has been since today's session opened. It plots directly on intraday price charts and resets at the start of each new session. Beginners often mistake a single day's session VWAP for the cost basis of all long-term holders.

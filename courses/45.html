Vortex 涡旋指标

我的学习与错题新手路线

记录仅保存在当前浏览器；已读不等于已经掌握。

Vortex（涡旋指标，通过相邻K线高低点交叉距离来衡量方向的指标）回答：最近相邻K线之间，是向上拉升的跨度更大，还是向下打压的跨度更大？它在副图中围绕1.0附近画出VI+和VI-两条线。新手常误以为VI+上穿VI-就是稳妥的买点，没注意到窄幅震荡时两线会密集交叉。

English introduction

The Vortex Indicator (a tool comparing cross-bar high-to-low distances) asks whether upward or downward movement between consecutive bars has been larger over a recent window. It plots VI+ and VI- oscillating around 1.0 in a lower pane. A common beginner mistake is treating every VI+/VI- crossover as a reliable trade signal while ignoring sideways noise.

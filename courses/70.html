English introduction

DPO (Detrended Price Oscillator, a tool comparing price with a shifted moving average to strip out broad trend and highlight historical short cycles) asks how many bars typically separate minor peaks and troughs once the main trend slope is removed. It plots around zero in a lower pane. The biggest beginner trap is treating Centered-mode peaks plotted in the past as real-time signals known on that bar.

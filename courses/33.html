RVOL回答：当前这根K线的成交量，是平时正常水平的几倍？它在副图以1.0倍为基准画柱子或曲线，读数为2代表成交量是基准的两倍。注意市面上有“比过去N根均量的普通RVOL”与“比过去N天同一钟点的同时段RVOL”两种版本。新手常把两个版本混为一谈。

English introduction

RVOL (Relative Volume, an indicator dividing current volume by average volume to express activity as a multiple of normal) asks how many times normal trading volume is occurring on the current bar. It plots around a 1.0 baseline in a lower pane, where 2.0 means twice the baseline volume. Beginners often fail to check whether a script compares against the prior N bars (ordinary RVOL) or against the same clock time on prior days (RVOL at Time).

KAMA（考夫曼自适应均线，根据走势顺畅程度自动调节反应快慢的均线）回答：如何在价格单边推进时跟得紧一点、而在价格原地乱跳时走得平缓一点？它是一条直接画在主图K线上的单根曲线。新手最常误解“自适应”三个字，以为它能聪明地消除所有滞后并躲开一切假突破。

English introduction

KAMA (Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average, a moving average that adjusts its speed based on directional efficiency) asks how to follow clean trends closely while slowing down during noisy sideways swings. It plots as a single line directly on the price chart. Beginners often misinterpret 'adaptive' as zero-lag prediction that avoids all whipsaws.

提出者与来源

Perry J. Kaufman — 官方资料将 KAMA 归于 Perry J. Kaufman；1995 年介绍。

Official documentation credits Perry J. Kaufman and its 1995 introduction.

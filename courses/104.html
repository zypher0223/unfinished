Advance/Decline Line回答：当前大盘指数的涨跌，到底是有大多数股票一起跟着涨，还是仅仅靠三五只超大市值巨头硬撑着指数？它在副图画一条累计线。新手常把它和第77课同样缩写为ADL的“累积/派发线”搞混。

English introduction

The Advance/Decline Line (A/D Line, a market-breadth indicator that adds each day's number of advancing stocks minus declining stocks to a running cumulative total) asks whether a stock-index move is supported by broad participation across many stocks or driven by just a few mega-cap heavyweights. It plots as a cumulative line in a lower pane. Beginners often confuse it with the volume-based Accumulation/Distribution Line (which shares the ADL acronym).

提出者与来源

1930年代市场广度实践；Richard Russell（推广） — TradingView 记载1930年代已有应用，1960年代因 Richard Russell 的 Dow Theory Letters 更受关注。另有二手资料称 Cleveland Trust 的 Leonard P. Ayres 早在1926年就开始计算和分析涨跌家数，《巴伦周刊》1931年起刊登这类数据；目前仍不宜断言唯一发明者。

TradingView records use in the 1930s and popularization through Richard Russell's Dow Theory Letters in the 1960s. Secondary sources say Leonard P. Ayres of Cleveland Trust analyzed advance-decline data as early as 1926 and Barron's published the figures from 1931; a sole inventor is still not firmly established.
